# The Wishing Spoon – Hypnotherapy & Holistic Wellness Website

The Wishing Spoon is a holistic wellness website designed to present personalized hypnotherapy services, spiritual guidance, and wellness products through a calm and empowering digital experience. The website reflects the brand’s focus on helping individuals reconnect with their inner confidence, release limiting patterns, and work toward meaningful personal transformation.

The website introduces services related to clinical hypnotherapy, past-life trauma and regression, weight-management support, smoking cessation, anxiety, depression, fears, phobias, grief, stress, and self-confidence. Clear service information and appointment calls to action help visitors understand the available support and begin their consultation journey.

Alongside its wellness services, the website features an online shop offering crystal and natural-stone bracelets, including Picasso Jasper, Morganite, Agatized Coral, Blue Apatite, Labradorite, African Turquoise, and Tiger’s Eye products. Product pricing, quick-view functionality, add-to-cart options, appointment scheduling, payment verification, practitioner credentials, and newsletter integration create a complete service and shopping platform.
