# LegalX – Startup & Business Legal Services Website

LegalX is a professional legal services website designed to provide startups and growing businesses with clear, practical, and affordable legal guidance. The website combines a modern corporate identity with structured service information, helping founders understand essential legal requirements while building, protecting, and scaling their companies.

The platform presents comprehensive business legal solutions, including entity selection, company formation, corporate compliance, governance documentation, operating agreements, bylaws, shareholder agreements, commercial contracts, and intellectual property guidance. Each service is clearly organized to simplify complex legal processes and help business owners make informed decisions.

The website also highlights specialized support for sales agreements, vendor contracts, partnerships, software licensing, risk management, cybersecurity, acquisitions, and successful business exits. Strong value messaging, flexible engagement options, professional expertise, and strategically placed consultation calls to action create a streamlined journey from exploring legal services to requesting professional support.
