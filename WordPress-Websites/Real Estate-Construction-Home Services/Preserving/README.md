# Preserving Home – Healthcare Training & Certification Website

Preserving Home is a professional healthcare training and certification website designed to support aspiring healthcare professionals as they prepare for certification and career advancement. The website presents CNA state testing, CPR certification training, and DODD medication certification through a clear, trustworthy, and encouraging digital experience.

The design focuses on making essential training information easy to understand and access. Dedicated service pages explain certification requirements, practical training, instructor support, flexible scheduling, and real-world preparation, helping visitors confidently select the program that matches their professional goals.

The website also introduces the organization’s healthcare background, compassionate mission, and commitment to supporting candidates throughout their certification journey. Appointment requests, course booking calls to action, upcoming events, news updates, testimonials, contact information, and newsletter features create a complete platform for student engagement and community outreach.
