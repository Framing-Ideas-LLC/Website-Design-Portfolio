# The Healthy Cocoon – Patient-Centered Healthcare Website

The Healthy Cocoon is a professional healthcare website designed to provide patients and families with a welcoming, accessible, and trustworthy digital experience. The website reflects the practice’s mission of delivering personalized, evidence-based primary care within an inclusive and safe environment for patients of all ages—from newborns to older adults.

The design presents a hybrid healthcare model that allows patients to choose between traditional insurance-based care and a direct primary care membership. Clear navigation, informative service sections, appointment scheduling, patient registration forms, portal access, and online bill payment help simplify the complete patient journey.

The website also highlights a broad range of healthcare services, including wellness visits, acute care, nutrition support, weight management, chronic disease management, mental health and medication support, blood testing, EKGs, biopsies, wound care, and minor in-office procedures. Accessibility controls and frequently asked questions make important healthcare information easier to understand and access across different devices.
