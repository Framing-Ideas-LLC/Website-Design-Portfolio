# Stormie Organizer & Tender

A clean and professional service-based website designed for a business offering home organization and bartending services. The website combines a minimal layout with bold orange accents, strong imagery, and clear calls to action to present both services in an engaging and easy-to-navigate way.

## Overview

The website introduces two core services: professional organization and bartending. The homepage is structured to quickly explain the business, showcase available services, introduce the founder, display previous work, build credibility through statistics, and encourage visitors to book a consultation.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with navigation, background imagery, and introductory messaging
* Welcome section explaining the brand and its purpose
* Service cards for decluttering, organization, bartending, and event cocktails
* About section featuring the business owner
* Organizing services portfolio and project showcase
* Consultation call-to-action section
* Business statistics and credibility section
* Bartending services portfolio
* Blog and helpful resources section
* Footer with navigation and branding

## Design Style

The website uses a modern and minimal visual style with a strong focus on whitespace and photography. Orange is used as the primary accent color for buttons, labels, and highlighted sections, while black and white provide a clean and professional foundation.

Typography combines bold modern headings with handwritten-style accent text, giving the website both a professional and personal feel.

## Purpose

The main goal of the website is to clearly communicate the company's organization and bartending services while building trust through portfolio imagery, experience statistics, and founder information. Strong calls to action throughout the page guide visitors toward booking a consultation or exploring the available services.
