### Mental Health - Psychiatry - Wellness

A collection of professional and reassuring websites designed for psychiatry practices, mental health clinics, therapists, and wellness providers. Each project combines a calm visual style, clear navigation, and responsive functionality to create a welcoming and trustworthy digital experience. From presenting psychiatric services, treatment options, and provider information to simplifying appointment booking and patient inquiries, these websites help healthcare professionals build credibility, connect with patients, and grow their practice online.
