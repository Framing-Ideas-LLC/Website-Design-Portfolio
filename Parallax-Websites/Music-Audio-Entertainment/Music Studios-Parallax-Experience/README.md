# Music Studios Parallax Experience

Music Studios is a modern parallax website concept designed to deliver an immersive and interactive digital experience for musicians, producers, and creative professionals. The project combines cinematic scrolling effects, smooth animations, and bold visual storytelling to create a dynamic journey that captures the energy of music and sound production.
