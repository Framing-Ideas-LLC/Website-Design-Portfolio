# Figma Mobile Application UI/UX Design Portfolio

A curated collection of mobile application UI/UX designs created in Figma, showcasing modern, user-focused, and visually engaging digital experiences. This portfolio highlights a range of projects across different industries, emphasizing intuitive user flows, clean interfaces, thoughtful interactions, and scalable design systems.

Each project follows a strategic design process—from research and wireframing to high-fidelity UI, prototyping, and usability-focused layouts. Every screen is designed to balance aesthetics with functionality, ensuring a seamless and engaging user experience across iOS and Android platforms.

### Portfolio Highlights

* Mobile App UI/UX Design
* User Research & UX Strategy
* Wireframes & User Flows
* Interactive Figma Prototypes
* Design Systems & Components
* iOS & Android Interfaces
* Modern Dashboard & SaaS Apps
* E-commerce & Marketplace Apps
* Healthcare, Finance & Business Solutions
* Clean, Minimal & Conversion-Focused Design

This portfolio reflects my approach to creating user-centered digital products that are not only visually refined but also practical, accessible, and built for real-world usability.

**Tools:** Figma • FigJam • Adobe Illustrator • Adobe Photoshop • Prototyping • Auto Layout • Components • Design Systems
