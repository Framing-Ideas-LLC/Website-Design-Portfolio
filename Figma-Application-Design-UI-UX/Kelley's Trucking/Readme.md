# Kelley's Trucking

### Trucking & Transportation Management App UI/UX Design

**Kelley's Trucking** is a mobile application concept designed to simplify trucking and transportation operations through a centralized digital experience. The app helps drivers, customers, and transportation teams manage loads, deliveries, routes, job assignments, and important shipment information more efficiently.

The goal was to create a professional, practical, and easy-to-use mobile experience that supports the daily workflow of a trucking business while keeping important information accessible and organized.

## Project Overview

The Kelley's Trucking app was designed with a focus on **efficiency, organization, communication, and reliable transportation management**.

The interface combines structured dashboards, clear navigation, shipment information, delivery tracking, driver-related features, and status-based workflows to help users manage transportation activities with less friction.



## Design Goals

* Simplify everyday trucking operations
* Make shipment and load information easy to access
* Improve visibility into deliveries and trip progress
* Organize driver and vehicle information
* Provide clear job assignment and status tracking
* Support better communication between users
* Create an efficient and scalable mobile experience
* Reduce complexity in transportation workflows

## Key Features

### Dashboard

A centralized dashboard provides an overview of active jobs, shipments, deliveries, upcoming activities, and important operational updates.

### Load & Shipment Management

Users can view and manage relevant load and shipment details, including assigned jobs, delivery information, and shipment status.

### Driver Management

Driver-related information is organized in a structured interface, making it easier to access assignments, trip details, and work-related updates.

### Trip & Route Information

The app provides organized trip and route details to help users understand upcoming destinations, delivery information, and transportation activities.

### Delivery Tracking

Users can monitor delivery progress and stay informed about the current status of assigned shipments and jobs.

### Job Assignments

A structured workflow allows transportation jobs to be assigned and reviewed, helping drivers and teams stay aligned.

### Notifications & Updates

Important shipment, job, and delivery updates are communicated through notifications, helping users stay informed throughout the workflow.

### History & Records

Completed trips, deliveries, and relevant activity records can be organized for easy reference and future review.

## Visual Direction

The visual direction focuses on **reliability, efficiency, clarity, and professionalism**. The interface uses structured layouts, strong typography, clear status indicators, recognizable icons, and organized information blocks to make transportation data easy to understand.

The design maintains a practical visual approach while ensuring important actions and information remain easily accessible during daily trucking operations.

## UX Approach

The Kelley's Trucking experience follows a straightforward operational journey:

**Dashboard → View Assignment → Review Load → Manage Trip → Track Delivery → Complete Job**

The workflow is designed to reduce unnecessary steps and provide users with the information they need at each stage of the transportation process.
