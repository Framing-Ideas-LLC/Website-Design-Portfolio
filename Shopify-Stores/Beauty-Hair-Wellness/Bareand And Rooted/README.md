# Bare & Rooted – Herbal Wellness Shopify Website

Bare & Rooted is a premium Shopify eCommerce website designed to showcase thoughtfully crafted herbal wellness and plant-based skincare products through a calm, natural, and trustworthy digital experience. The website reflects the brand’s philosophy that healing begins at the root, combining organic-inspired visuals with clear messaging focused on everyday wellness and intentional self-care.

The store presents a curated selection of herbal teas, skincare essentials, wellness oils, and product bundles created around concerns such as stress and sleep, women’s wellness, digestion, hormone support, and skincare. Clear product categories, featured customer favorites, pricing information, availability filters, and direct add-to-cart options make product discovery simple and convenient.

The website also communicates the personal story behind the brand through a dedicated founder section, helping customers understand its nature-focused values and commitment to small-batch, carefully selected products. Frequently asked questions, customer reviews, international currency selection, newsletter signup, account access, and a streamlined checkout journey further strengthen trust and usability.
