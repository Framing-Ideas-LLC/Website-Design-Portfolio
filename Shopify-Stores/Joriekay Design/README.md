# Jorie Kay Design – Sustainable Fashion Shopify Website

Jorie Kay Design is a creative Shopify eCommerce website developed to showcase one-of-a-kind fashion pieces made from reclaimed and thrifted materials. The brand transforms pre-loved textiles and graphic apparel into distinctive bags, tops, dresses, ponchos, and handmade accessories, combining sustainable values with bold, expressive style.

The website is designed around a visually engaging and easy-to-explore shopping experience. Clear product categories allow customers to browse crochet pieces, tote and messenger bags, reworked tops, dresses, ponchos, seasonal collections, and sale items without unnecessary complexity. Strong product imagery and direct shopping calls-to-action help each handmade design receive the attention it deserves.

Every section supports the brand’s “Not Vintage. Reborn.” philosophy by presenting reclaimed materials as modern, wearable, and meaningful fashion. The store structure also includes customer accounts, cart functionality, international region selection, newsletter signup, and a streamlined checkout journey designed for customers across different devices and locations.
