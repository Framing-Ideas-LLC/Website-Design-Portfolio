# Parsley Hauling

A modern, bold, and responsive hauling service website designed for Parsley Hauling LLC. The website focuses on showcasing reliable hauling services, building customer trust, and generating quote requests through a clean and professional layout.

## Features

* Full-screen hero section with strong branding and call-to-action
* Service overview for commercial and residential hauling
* Dedicated sections for hauling and debris removal services
* Company values and service highlights
* Simple step-by-step process for requesting a quote
* Strong call-to-action sections throughout the website
* Contact form for project and quote inquiries
* Responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
* Dark industrial design with bold typography and construction imagery

## Sections

* Hero
* Company Introduction
* Services
* Service Highlights
* Why Choose Us
* Work Process
* Call To Action
* Contact
* Footer

## Design

The website uses a dark industrial visual style with high-contrast typography, red accent colors, large construction imagery, and strong visual hierarchy. The layout is designed to communicate reliability, professionalism, and heavy-duty hauling services.
