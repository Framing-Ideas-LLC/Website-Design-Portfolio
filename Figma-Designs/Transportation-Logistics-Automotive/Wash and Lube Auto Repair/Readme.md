# Mobile Auto Repair Website

A modern and responsive website design for a mobile auto repair service. The website focuses on building trust, presenting repair services clearly, and making it easy for customers to request a mechanic.

## Overview

The design uses a dark automotive-style hero section with strong typography, gold accents, and real service imagery. The layout is structured to guide visitors from the main service introduction to service details, previous work, company information, service areas, and the booking form.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with mobile auto repair messaging and service call-to-actions
* Service statistics and trust indicators
* Services section covering different automotive repair solutions
* Work gallery showcasing completed repair projects
* About Us section with company information and experience
* Benefits section explaining why customers should choose the service
* Service area section
* Mobile mechanic booking call-to-action
* Request a Service section with contact details and inquiry form
* Footer with navigation and business information

## Design Style

The website uses a professional automotive visual style with dark backgrounds, warm gold accents, bold condensed headings, clean content areas, and real automotive imagery. The layout is designed to maintain a strong balance between visual impact and practical information.
