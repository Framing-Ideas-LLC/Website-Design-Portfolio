# Green Movers Website

A modern and professional moving service website designed with a clean, trustworthy, and premium visual style. The website focuses on making the relocation process feel simple, reliable, and stress-free.

## Overview

The homepage introduces the moving company with a strong hero section, clear messaging, service highlights, and an easy quote request option. The design uses spacious layouts, professional imagery, subtle visual elements, and a green color palette that reflects reliability, growth, and sustainability.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a moving professional image and strong headline
* Navigation with services, process, reviews, and contact links
* Company statistics highlighting experience and customer satisfaction
* About section introducing the company's professional approach
* Service portfolio for residential moving, office relocation, packing, and commercial moving
* Benefits section explaining why customers should choose the company
* Step-by-step moving process from first consultation to final delivery
* Customer reviews and satisfaction ratings
* Final call-to-action section for requesting a moving quote
* Detailed footer with navigation, company information, and contact details

## Design Style

The website combines a light and clean interface with dark green content sections for visual contrast. Large editorial typography is paired with modern sans-serif text, while subtle yellow and green accents are used to highlight important information and calls to action.
