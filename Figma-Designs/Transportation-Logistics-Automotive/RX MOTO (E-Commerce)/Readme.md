# RX MOTO E-Commerce Website

RX MOTO is a modern sportswear and lifestyle e-commerce website designed for active individuals who value performance, comfort, and bold street-inspired fashion. The website combines strong product photography, clean typography, and a minimal black, white, and orange visual style to create a premium shopping experience.

## Key Sections

* Full-width hero banner with promotional messaging
* Brand values and key benefits section
* Product showcase with apparel and accessories
* About the brand section with lifestyle imagery
* Sports footwear promotional banner
* Product categories for jackets, riding tees, sweatshirts, hoodies, gloves, and caps
* Customer testimonial section
* Featured product collection
* Contact and inquiry form
* Latest trends and articles section
* Detailed footer with navigation and contact information

## Design Style

The website uses a bold and performance-focused visual direction. Large uppercase typography, high-quality lifestyle photography, spacious layouts, and strong contrast give the interface a modern sportswear identity. Orange accents are used selectively for buttons and important interactive elements.

## Color Palette

* Black
* White
* Light Gray
* Orange Accent

## User Experience

The layout guides visitors naturally from brand introduction to product discovery, brand information, customer feedback, and contact options. Product cards are organized in a clean grid, while promotional sections and lifestyle imagery help maintain visual interest throughout the page.

## Purpose

The main purpose of the website is to establish RX MOTO as a premium activewear and lifestyle brand while providing customers with a simple and engaging way to explore apparel, footwear, accessories, and related brand content.
