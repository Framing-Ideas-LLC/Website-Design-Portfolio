# Markeida Faithe – The Power Pusher

## Project Description

A modern and elegant personal brand website designed for Markeida Faithe, focused on spiritual healing, personal growth, faith, and empowerment. The website creates a strong and calming experience through bold typography, emotional imagery, and a clean editorial-style layout.

The homepage introduces the brand with a powerful hero section and clear navigation to services, spiritual resources, and personal guidance. It includes sections for healing services, personalized support, spiritual education, professional foundations, resources, testimonials, frequently asked questions, and newsletter signup.

The visual design combines a soft neutral background with dark imagery and bold red accent buttons. Large serif-style headings create a confident and premium feel, while high-quality portraits and lifestyle imagery help establish an emotional connection with visitors.

## Key Features

* Personal brand and spiritual wellness website
* Full-width hero section with strong visual storytelling
* About section introducing Markeida Faithe
* Healing and spiritual guidance services
* Support and healing journey section
* Professional and faith-based foundation content
* Books and digital resources section
* Client testimonials
* FAQ section for new visitors
* Newsletter subscription form
* Responsive layout for desktop and mobile devices
* Clean navigation and clear call-to-action buttons

## Design Style

The website uses a modern, minimal, and editorial-inspired design approach. Neutral backgrounds, dark overlays, expressive photography, bold condensed typography, and red accent colors create a professional yet emotionally engaging visual identity.
