# McGill's Lawn & Garden Service

A clean and modern landscaping website designed to showcase professional lawn care, gardening, and outdoor maintenance services. The layout uses a fresh green and white visual style with strong imagery, clear service sections, and simple calls to action.

## Overview

The website provides homeowners with an easy way to explore landscaping services, learn about the company, view completed projects, understand the working process, and request a quote.

The design focuses on simplicity, trust, and professional presentation while maintaining a natural outdoor aesthetic.

## Main Sections

* Responsive navigation with Contact Us CTA
* Hero section with gardening imagery and Request for Quote CTA
* Featured service categories
* About company section with experience highlight
* Lawn and garden services overview
* Features and service benefits
* Recent projects and portfolio gallery
* Project category filtering
* Four-step working process
* Gardening news and articles
* Quote call-to-action section
* Informative footer with important links and contact details

## Services Highlighted

The website presents several landscaping and garden maintenance services, including:

* Trimming
* Grass Mowing
* Weed Removal
* Seasonal Planting
* Garden Renovation
* Outdoor Maintenance

## Design Style

The interface uses a natural green color palette combined with white backgrounds and subtle light-green sections. Rounded imagery, spacious layouts, large photography, and minimal decorative elements help create a professional yet approachable landscaping brand.

Typography is modern and easy to read, with bold headings used to establish a clear visual hierarchy.

## User Experience

The page is structured to guide visitors naturally from discovering the company to exploring services and reviewing previous work. Clear CTA buttons such as "Request for Quote", "Learn More", "View More", and "Contact Us" make important actions easy to find.
