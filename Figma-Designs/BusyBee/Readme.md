# Busy Bees Specialized Care

A responsive healthcare website designed for **Busy Bees Specialized Care**, focused on presenting personalized care services in a clean, professional, and welcoming layout.

## Features

* Hero section with healthcare-focused messaging and primary call-to-action
* About Us section introducing the care team
* Services section highlighting specialized care offerings
* Help section explaining how the organization supports patients
* Appointment scheduling section with a clear call-to-action
* Blog section for healthcare-related articles and resources
* Testimonials section for client feedback
* Free consultation contact form
* Structured footer with navigation and contact information
* Clean healthcare-focused visual design with soft green and neutral tones
* Responsive layout suitable for desktop and mobile devices

## Design

* Primary color palette based on dark green, olive green, soft sage, and white
* Serif typography used for major headings to create a warm and trustworthy feel
* Clean sans-serif typography for supporting content
* Professional healthcare imagery combined with transparent background staff images
* Soft textured backgrounds used across selected sections
* Rounded cards and subtle borders for service, blog, and testimonial content
* Clear spacing and section hierarchy for easy navigation
