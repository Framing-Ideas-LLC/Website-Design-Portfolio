# Black God Comics Landing Page

## Overview

Black God Comics is a bold and artistic landing page designed for a comic and visual storytelling brand. The website focuses on comic artwork, character design, storyboarding, and illustration services.

The design combines a clean editorial layout with strong red typography, black sections, and hand-drawn comic artwork to create a distinctive visual identity.

## Key Features

* Large typography-driven hero section
* Comic artwork and illustration showcase
* Storyboarding and character design sections
* Services overview
* Selected works portfolio section
* Featured comic artwork
* Statistics and client information
* Call-to-action sections
* Insights and creative articles section
* Contact and newsletter area
* Multi-column footer navigation

## Design Style

* Editorial and artistic visual direction
* Strong black, white, and red color palette
* Large serif display typography
* Hand-drawn comic illustrations
* High contrast layouts
* Mix of clean white sections and dark portfolio sections
* Asymmetrical image compositions
* Bold typography used as a major visual element

## Purpose

The landing page is designed to showcase Black God Comics as a creative studio specializing in comic art, storytelling, character design, storyboarding, and illustration. The visual-heavy layout helps establish the brand's artistic personality while directing visitors toward the portfolio and contact sections.
