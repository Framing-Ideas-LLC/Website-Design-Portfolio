# Mister Check Website

## Project Overview
Mister Check is a professional security consulting website focused on business security assessments, risk management, and security readiness. The website presents the company's expertise, services, and approach to helping businesses identify vulnerabilities and improve their security.

## Key Features
- Professional homepage with a hero section and clear call-to-action buttons.
- Security services section featuring assessments, training, architecture reviews, and compliance readiness.
- Company introduction highlighting investigative experience and local expertise.
- Founder story and professional background section.
- Three-step process explaining the company's working principles.
- FAQ section addressing common client questions.
- Service coverage section highlighting locations across Virginia.
- Contact section with a consultation call-to-action.
- Responsive, modern layout with a navy blue, gold, and white color scheme.

## Project Deliverables
- Homepage UI design.
- Service cards and content sections.
- Company profile and methodology sections.
- FAQ and service area sections.
- Contact and consultation sections.
- Website footer with navigation and business information.

## Design Approach
The design uses a clean, professional layout with strong typography, structured content, dark blue backgrounds, and gold accents. The visual style communicates trust, expertise, and reliability while guiding visitors toward requesting a consultation.
