# Junk Journals

A premium, editorial-style website designed for a handcrafted junk journal brand. The layout combines warm earthy tones, elegant typography, handcrafted imagery, and a clean product-focused structure.

## Features

* Full-width hero section with strong headline and call-to-action
* Introduction section highlighting the journaling experience
* Featured products and handcrafted journal collections
* Product showcase with category-based browsing
* Featured projects and creative craftsmanship sections
* Brand philosophy and statistics section
* Customer testimonial section
* Shop by category section
* Lifestyle image gallery
* Recent news and journal articles section
* Newsletter and detailed footer area
* Responsive layout for desktop and mobile devices

## Design

The design uses a warm, handcrafted aesthetic with neutral backgrounds, brown and beige accents, editorial typography, and photography focused on journals, paper textures, craftsmanship, and creative storytelling.


## Purpose

The website is designed to showcase handcrafted journals, communicate the brand story, highlight craftsmanship, and guide visitors toward exploring products and making a purchase.
