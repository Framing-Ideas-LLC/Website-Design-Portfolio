# Corris Consulting & Design

A modern and professional website designed for a MEP engineering and building design company.

The website focuses on presenting HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and coordinated MEP engineering services through a clean and structured user experience.

## Project Overview

Corris Consulting & Design is a corporate website created for a professional engineering company specializing in complete MEP solutions for residential and commercial projects.

The design combines dark industrial visuals with clean white content sections to create a professional and technical appearance.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong introduction and call-to-action
* Overview of HVAC, electrical, and plumbing engineering services
* About section introducing the company's expertise
* Full-width call-to-action section
* Service cards for different MEP engineering solutions
* Clear engineering process section
* Newsletter subscription area
* Multi-column footer with company and contact information

## Services

The website highlights the following services:

* HVAC Design & Load Calculations
* Electrical System Design
* Plumbing System Design
* Revit Drafting & Coordination
* MEP Coordination
* Residential and Commercial Engineering Solutions

## Design Style

The visual direction is modern, minimal, technical, and professional.

The website uses:

* Dark industrial backgrounds
* High-quality engineering and construction imagery
* Clean typography
* Large section spacing
* Structured content layouts
* Minimal call-to-action buttons
* A strong contrast between dark and light sections
