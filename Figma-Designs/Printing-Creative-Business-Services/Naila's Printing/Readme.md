# Naila's Printing – Custom Printing Service Website

A modern and responsive website design created for **Naila's Printing**, a custom printing business offering personalized printing solutions for apparel, mugs, packaging, and promotional products. The design focuses on providing a clean user experience, showcasing products effectively, and encouraging customer engagement through strong call-to-actions.

## Project Overview

This landing page is designed to highlight the company's printing services, product categories, packaging options, and customer testimonials while maintaining a professional and visually appealing interface.

## Features

* Modern and responsive landing page
* Clean hero section with featured products
* Custom product design showcase
* Printing service highlights
* Packaging preview section
* Product category tabs
* Featured products section
* Promotional banner
* Service category cards
* Customer testimonials slider
* Frequently Asked Questions section
* Professional footer with navigation links

## Design Highlights

* Minimal and business-focused layout
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Balanced whitespace for improved readability
* High-quality product imagery
* Consistent blue and white color palette
* Clear typography for better user experience
* Well-structured content sections
* Conversion-focused call-to-action buttons
