# KWJFL – Key West Junior Football League

A modern and energetic website concept designed for Key West Junior Football League. The website uses a strong sports-focused visual style with dark backgrounds, bold typography, red accent colors, and high-impact football imagery to create a professional and community-driven experience.

## Project Overview

The website is designed to represent a youth football organization while encouraging young athletes, parents, coaches, and volunteers to become part of the community.

The overall design focuses on:

* Strong visual storytelling
* Youth football and team spirit
* Easy access to registration and team information
* Clear calls to action
* Community and volunteer engagement
* Mobile-friendly user experience

## Hero Section

The homepage opens with a full-screen football action image featuring young players.

The hero includes:

* Main navigation
* League branding and logo
* Large headline focused on building champions
* Supporting description
* Primary registration call to action

A dark overlay improves text readability while keeping the football imagery as the main visual focus.

## About Section

This section introduces the mission of the Key West Junior Football League.

It combines:

* A short introduction about empowering youth
* Supporting bullet points
* A large lifestyle football image

The layout creates a balanced combination of information and emotional storytelling.

## Training and Development

A dedicated section highlights the benefits of joining the league.

The content is presented through three feature cards focused on areas such as:

* Skill development
* Leadership
* Community and teamwork

The center card uses a red background to create visual emphasis and establish hierarchy.

## Join Our Team

This section encourages visitors to become involved with the organization.

It includes:

* Action-focused football imagery
* Information about becoming a coach or volunteer
* Supporting benefits
* Clear calls to action

The design makes it easy for potential volunteers to understand how they can participate.

## Registration Section

A dark, image-based section is used to promote game day registration.

The section includes:

* Registration information
* Team or program options
* Important dates
* Multiple registration cards

The layered card design allows users to quickly explore available programs and registration options.

## Gallery Section

The gallery showcases memorable moments from youth football games and events.

The section uses an overlapping image composition instead of a traditional grid, creating a more dynamic and editorial appearance.

A simple slider control allows visitors to browse through additional moments.

## Sponsors Section

A dedicated area highlights sponsors and community partners.

The section includes:

* Sponsor introduction
* Partnership benefits
* Multiple sponsorship categories
* Clear sponsor-related calls to action

A large football player visual is integrated into the layout to maintain the energetic sports identity.

## Testimonials

The testimonial section shares experiences from parents and members of the football community.

Each testimonial card includes:

* Rating indicators
* Short feedback
* Member information

The carousel layout allows multiple testimonials to be displayed while maintaining a clean and structured design.

## Newsletter and Final CTA

A strong closing section encourages visitors to stay connected with the league.

It includes:

* A football team background image
* Email subscription field
* Call to action button

The section provides a clear conversion point before the footer.

## Footer

The footer contains:

* League logo
* Main navigation links
* Copyright information
* Website credits

The dark footer maintains consistency with the overall sports-focused visual identity.

## Design Style

The visual direction is bold, energetic, and community-focused.

Key design characteristics include:

* Dark and dramatic backgrounds
* Strong red accent color
* Bold condensed typography
* High-quality football photography
* Large headlines
* Rounded cards and buttons
* Clean white content sections
* Strong contrast between sections

## Key Features

* Responsive website design
* Youth football-focused visual identity
* Registration call-to-action areas
* Volunteer and coaching opportunities
* Sponsor promotion
* Testimonial slider
* Football image gallery
* Newsletter signup
* Clear navigation structure
