# Lady Royals HTX

Lady Royals HTX is a modern and energetic sports community website designed for a youth cheer and athletics organization. The website focuses on showcasing the team's achievements, building community engagement, promoting events, and providing clear information about memberships and monthly dues.

## Overview

The website uses a clean layout with a strong purple, gold, white, and black color palette that reflects the team's identity. Large photography, bold typography, and structured content sections create an energetic and community-focused experience.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with the main message and call-to-action
* Champion and team showcase
* Featured achievements and competition highlights
* Full-width promotional section for joining the team
* Commitment and training gallery
* Statistics and community impact section
* Team photo carousel
* Monthly dues and membership information
* Parent testimonial section
* Latest news and insights
* Event promotion section
* Footer with navigation and contact information

## Design Features

* Bold sports-focused visual identity
* Purple and gold brand accents
* Large team and competition photography
* Clean card-based layouts
* Responsive content structure
* Clear call-to-action buttons
* Image galleries and team showcases
* Membership and pricing section
* Testimonial and news sections
