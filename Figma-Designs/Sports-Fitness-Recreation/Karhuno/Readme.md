## KARHUNO – AI-Powered Sales Intelligence Website

KARHUNO is a modern AI-powered B2B sales platform designed to help businesses discover, monitor, and qualify potential leads using social and news signals. The website presents KARHUNO’s services through a clean, futuristic SaaS-style interface focused on lead generation and sales automation.

### Website Overview

The landing page introduces KARHUNO’s AI-driven sales solution with a strong hero section, clear calls to action, service explanations, case studies, pricing options, and a free trial flow. The overall structure is designed to guide visitors from understanding the product to starting a trial or requesting a demo.

### Key Sections

* Hero section with product positioning and Start Trial CTA
* Target audience and business use-case introduction
* Client and partner showcase
* Signal Monitoring and Lead Profiling services
* Step-by-step explanation of how each service works
* Benefits of using KARHUNO AI
* Visual sales workflow/process diagram
* Case studies showcasing real-world applications
* Pricing plans for Signal Monitoring and Lead Profiling
* Free trial options based on different business needs
* Request Trial form for lead generation
* Detailed footer with navigation and contact information

### Core Features

* AI-powered social and news signal monitoring
* Automated lead discovery
* Lead profiling and verification
* Weekly delivery of relevant sales opportunities
* Custom lead selection based on business requirements
* Support for multiple languages
* Sales workflow automation
* Higher conversion potential through targeted leads
* Reduced manual research and prospecting time

### Design Style

The website uses a dark, futuristic SaaS design with deep navy and purple backgrounds. Bright purple gradients are used throughout the interface for buttons, cards, borders, and highlighted elements.

The layout combines large amounts of whitespace with structured content blocks, helping maintain readability despite the amount of information presented. Rounded cards, subtle borders, gradient backgrounds, and circular graphics reinforce the AI and technology-focused visual identity.

### Color Palette

### Responsive Design

The website structure can be adapted for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Multi-column sections, service cards, pricing plans, and process flows can stack vertically on smaller screens while maintaining clear content hierarchy and accessible CTA placement.
