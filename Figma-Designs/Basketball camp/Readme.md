# Elite Hoops Basketball Camp

A modern basketball camp website designed to promote training programs, showcase coaching expertise, and make camp registration simple for players and parents.

## Features

* Hero section with basketball-focused branding and call-to-action
* Camp details and schedule
* Coach and training information
* Skills development and training categories
* Online registration form
* Player testimonials
* Payment method section
* Action shots and training gallery
* Frequently asked questions
* Responsive footer navigation

## Design

The website uses a dark navy, white, blue, and pink color palette with bold sports-focused typography and high-energy basketball imagery. The layout is designed to feel professional, competitive, and engaging.
