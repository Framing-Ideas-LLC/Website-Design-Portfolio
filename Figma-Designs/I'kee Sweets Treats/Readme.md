# I'kee Sweets Treats

A modern bakery and cake shop website designed to showcase handcrafted cakes, desserts, and sweet treats. The website combines a soft, elegant visual style with product-focused sections to create a warm and inviting customer experience.

## Features

* Full-width hero section with seasonal bakery imagery
* Brand introduction and company story section
* Service highlights for quality assurance, innovative solutions, and customer assistance
* Product showcase featuring cakes and desserts with pricing
* Featured and most-demanded cake sections
* Category-based dessert discovery section
* Bakery story and experience section
* Customer statistics and business highlights
* Customer contact form
* Customer testimonial section
* Newsletter subscription area
* Responsive navigation and structured footer

## Design

The design uses a clean white layout with purple and pink accents, decorative floral elements, large food photography, and elegant typography. Product images are presented prominently to keep the focus on the bakery's offerings.
