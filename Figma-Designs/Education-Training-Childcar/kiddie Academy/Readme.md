# Kiddie Academy Website

A modern and engaging childcare and early learning website designed to create a welcoming experience for parents and families. The website uses a warm combination of orange, beige, white, and green tones to create a friendly, playful, and trustworthy visual identity.

## Overview

The website presents information about the academy, its educational approach, services, programs, facilities, and activities. The design focuses on clear communication, engaging visuals, and easy navigation.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a welcoming introduction and call-to-action button
* About section introducing the academy and its learning approach
* Feature cards highlighting safe, engaging, and educational experiences
* Summer programs section with activity cards
* Child development and learning information
* Indoor and outdoor sports activities
* Statistics and achievements section
* Projects and educational initiatives
* Age-based learning programs
* Image gallery showcasing children and academy activities
* Contact form for parents and inquiries
* Footer with navigation links, resources, and contact details

## Design Style

The website follows a playful and modern educational design. Soft backgrounds, rounded cards, curved section dividers, and child-focused imagery create a warm and approachable experience. The orange accents highlight important content and calls to action, while the green sections add contrast and visual balance.

## Technologies

* HTML
* CSS
* JavaScript
* Responsive Design

## Purpose

The goal of the website is to provide parents with clear information about the academy while showcasing its educational programs, activities, learning environment, and child-focused approach.
