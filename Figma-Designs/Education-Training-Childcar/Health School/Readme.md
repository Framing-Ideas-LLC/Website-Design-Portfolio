# Health School Website

## Overview

A modern and responsive healthcare education website designed for a medical school or healthcare training institute. The website presents educational programs, experienced doctors, campus information, services, student resources, and admission-related content in a clean and professional layout.

## Features

* Responsive and modern website design
* Healthcare and medical education focused interface
* Hero section with admission call-to-action
* School benefits and key highlights
* About Us section
* Personalized learning plans
* Workshops and seminars information
* Clinical training and career counseling
* Medical care and student support services
* Campus life section
* Instructor and faculty showcase
* Academic programs with category tabs
* Student testimonials section
* Contact and admission call-to-action
* Newsletter subscription form
* Complete footer with navigation and contact information

## Pages and Sections

* Home
* About Us
* Admissions
* Gallery
* Programs
* Academic Programs
* Campus Life
* Instructors
* Testimonials
* Contact

## Design Style

The design uses a clean white and light gray background combined with a dark navy blue accent color. Medical imagery, structured cards, modern typography, and spacious layouts create a professional and trustworthy experience suitable for a healthcare education platform.
