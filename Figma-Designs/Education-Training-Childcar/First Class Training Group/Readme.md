## FCTG – Training & Education Organization Website (Figma UI/UX)

**FCTG** is a modern education and training website concept designed in **Figma** to connect students and employers with specialized training providers that support career development and workforce growth. The design positions FCTG as a trusted parent organization bringing together quality training, industry expertise, and clear pathways toward professional success.

The website is crafted to showcase FCTG's two key brands, **RTS QLD** and **First Class Training**, while clearly communicating their respective training offerings and areas of expertise. Professional layouts, confident typography, structured content, and intuitive navigation create a credible digital experience for students, employers, and training partners.
