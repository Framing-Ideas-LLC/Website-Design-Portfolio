# Milestones 4 Kidz Website

A modern and child-friendly website design created for **Milestones 4 Kidz**, a therapy and developmental support service for children. The website focuses on creating a warm, welcoming, and trustworthy experience for parents looking for professional support for their children.

## Project Overview

The website presents the brand's services in a clear and engaging way while using playful visual elements that reflect a child-focused environment. The design balances friendly illustrations, real photography, soft colors, and structured content to make the website approachable without losing its professional feel.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a clear message and call-to-action
* Overview of therapy and child development services
* Speech Therapy
* Language Therapy
* Autism and Neurodiversity Support
* Feeding Therapy
* Informative section explaining the brand's approach
* Benefits and key features section
* Family-focused call-to-action section
* Parent testimonials and reviews
* Simple navigation and contact options
* Footer with important links and social media access

## Design Approach

The visual style uses a soft and playful color palette combined with blue, yellow, orange, and red accents. Hand-drawn shapes, illustrations, curved elements, and child-friendly graphics help create an engaging experience.

Real images of children and families are used throughout the website to build trust and create an emotional connection with parents.

The layout is designed with clear content hierarchy, spacious sections, and strategically placed call-to-action buttons to guide visitors toward learning more or getting in touch.

## Goal

The goal of the website is to help parents easily understand the available services, build confidence in the Milestones 4 Kidz team, and encourage them to take the first step toward supporting their child's growth and development.
