# Alleviation Associates Tax Debt Professionals

A professional tax resolution and training website designed for Alleviation Associates, a tax debt resolution agency.

## Overview

The website promotes tax resolution services, referral agent opportunities, tax resolution training programs, and educational resources. The design uses a professional corporate layout with strong typography, structured content sections, blue accent colors, and image-based service presentations.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with tax resolution messaging and primary call-to-action
* Tax resolution services overview
* Referral Agent Program
* Become a Referral Agent in 4 Easy Steps
* Course Curriculum
* Tax Resolution Training
* Tax Resolution Individual Training
* Frequently Asked Questions
* IRS Call List resource section
* Why Work With Alleviation
* Founder and CEO introduction
* Footer with company information and quick links

## Design Features

* Clean and professional business-focused layout
* Blue, white, gray, and black color palette
* Bold condensed typography for headings
* Image-based service and training sections
* Strong CTA buttons throughout the page
* Alternating content layouts for visual variety
* Responsive-style section structure
* FAQ accordion layout
* Promotional sections for training and referral programs
