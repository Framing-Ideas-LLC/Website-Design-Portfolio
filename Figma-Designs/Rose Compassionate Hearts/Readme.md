# Roses & Hearts

Roses & Hearts is a responsive website design for a senior home care and caregiver service. The design focuses on creating a warm, trustworthy, and compassionate experience for seniors and their families.

## Features

* Clean and responsive homepage design
* Hero section with strong brand messaging
* Senior home care introduction section
* Trust and credibility statistics
* Personalized care services section
* Caregiver and senior-focused imagery
* Reasons to choose the care service
* Simple step-by-step care process
* Family testimonials section
* Call-to-action section
* Frequently Asked Questions section
* Contact and service information
* Structured footer navigation

## Design

The design uses a soft pink and cream color palette combined with burgundy accents. Large photography, rounded cards, elegant typography, and spacious layouts create a warm and reassuring visual experience.
