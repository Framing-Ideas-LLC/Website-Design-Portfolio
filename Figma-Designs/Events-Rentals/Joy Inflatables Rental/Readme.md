## Joy Inflatables – Party & Event Rental Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Joy Inflatables** is a colorful and family-friendly party rental website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase exciting entertainment options for birthdays, school celebrations, community events, and everyday fun. Serving Pensacola, Cantonment, Pace, Milton, Gulf Breeze, Perdido, Florida, along with Seminole and Baldwin County, Alabama, the brand focuses on making every rental experience simple, enjoyable, and stress-free.

The website is crafted to highlight bounce houses, water and dry slides, tables, chairs, concession machines, rental packages, service areas, and booking options. Bright event photography, playful typography, vibrant layouts, and clear calls to action create an engaging digital experience that captures the fun and family-friendly personality of Joy Inflatables.
