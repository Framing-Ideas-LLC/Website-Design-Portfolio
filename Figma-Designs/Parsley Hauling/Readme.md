# Parsley Hauling LLC Website

A modern website design for Parsley Hauling LLC, focused on commercial and residential hauling services.

## Overview

The website uses a dark, industrial visual style with high-contrast typography, truck imagery, and red accent colors to create a strong and reliable brand presence.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with primary call-to-action
* Company introduction section
* Commercial and residential hauling services
* Service overview with multiple hauling categories
* Company benefits and key differentiators
* Process section explaining how the service works
* Strong CTA section for quote requests
* Contact and inquiry section
* Footer with company information and navigation

## Design Style

* Dark industrial theme
* Bold typography
* High-contrast layouts
* Red accent color
* Large construction and hauling imagery
* Clean service-focused layouts
* Conversion-focused call-to-action sections
