## Ayanaz – Restaurant & Food Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Ayanaz** is a modern restaurant website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase a fresh, locally inspired dining experience built around quality, authenticity, and family tradition. The design reflects the brand's commitment to preparing every meal with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and handmade care.

The website is crafted to highlight the menu, signature dishes, ingredients, restaurant story, family recipes, and dining experience while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm food photography, inviting layouts, elegant typography, and intuitive navigation create an appetizing digital experience that communicates the heart behind every dish.
