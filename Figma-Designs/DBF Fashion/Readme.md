# DBF Fashion Website

A modern fashion e-commerce website designed to showcase clothing collections, trending products, and style-focused content. The layout uses a clean editorial approach with a minimal color palette, large product imagery, and structured sections to create a premium shopping experience.

## Overview

The website is designed around a complete fashion shopping journey, starting with a promotional hero section and continuing through featured collections, product listings, style inspiration, promotional banners, articles, and a detailed footer.

## Key Sections

* Top announcement bar with promotional information
* Clean navigation with logo, menu links, search, account, and shopping options
* Large hero section featuring a fashion model and primary campaign message
* Promotional category section highlighting fashion occasions and recommended styles
* Featured clothing collection with product cards, prices, and quick action buttons
* Editorial fashion section combining lifestyle imagery and brand-focused content
* Promotional banner encouraging users to discover their personal style
* Trending products and popular fashion picks displayed in a structured product grid
* Large promotional image banners for featured clothing categories and campaigns
* Latest articles section for fashion inspiration and styling content
* Newsletter subscription area
* Multi-column footer with quick links, customer service information, and brand details

## Design Style

The design follows a clean, modern, and fashion-focused visual direction. Large lifestyle photography is combined with minimal typography, soft neutral backgrounds, structured grids, and generous spacing.

The product cards and editorial sections create a balanced mix between e-commerce functionality and fashion storytelling, helping users explore products while maintaining a premium brand experience.
