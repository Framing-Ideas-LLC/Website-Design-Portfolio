# Boutique Facial Aesthetics

A premium facial aesthetics website designed with a clean, elegant, and luxury-focused visual style.

## Overview

The website presents a boutique aesthetic clinic with a strong focus on natural beauty, premium treatments, and a personalized client experience. The design combines warm neutral tones, refined typography, high-quality imagery, and spacious layouts to create a sophisticated look.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong brand introduction and primary calls to action
* Beauty and treatment introduction section
* Signature treatments grid
* Boutique Difference section with service highlights
* Before and After gallery
* Client testimonials
* Appointment booking section with contact form
* Footer with quick links, treatments, and location details

## Design Features

* Elegant luxury aesthetic
* Warm beige and neutral color palette
* Serif typography paired with clean supporting text
* Large editorial-style imagery
* Minimal and spacious layouts
* Clear call-to-action buttons
* Structured treatment cards
* Responsive-friendly section structure
* Premium healthcare and beauty brand presentation
