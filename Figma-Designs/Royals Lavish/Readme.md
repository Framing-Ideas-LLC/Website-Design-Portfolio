## Royals Lavish Hair Boutique – Luxury Hair E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Royals Lavish Hair Boutique** is a premium hair e-commerce website concept designed in **Figma** to create a luxurious, empowering, and personalized shopping experience for women. Founded in 2023 by Candy Boston, the brand goes beyond selling hair by creating an experience centered around confidence, beauty, individuality, and the belief that every woman deserves to feel like royalty.

The website is crafted to highlight curated hair bundles, premium collections, product details, styling inspiration, customer care, and the brand story while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Elegant typography, sophisticated layouts, premium product imagery, and feminine visual elements create a polished digital experience that reflects the boutique's luxurious identity.
