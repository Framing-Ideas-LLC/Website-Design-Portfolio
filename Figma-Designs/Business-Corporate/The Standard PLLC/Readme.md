# The Standard PLLC

A modern and professional website designed for The Standard PLLC, focused on community empowerment, mental health support, and accessible care.

## Project Overview

The website presents a clean and welcoming digital experience with a strong focus on trust, support, and community connection. The design uses soft colors, spacious layouts, and human-centered imagery to create a calm and approachable experience.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a clear introduction and call-to-action
* About section introducing the organization and its mission
* Mission and values section highlighting core principles
* Vision section explaining long-term goals
* Services section covering equitable access, positive outcomes, at-risk support, health stabilization, and ending discrimination
* Psychotherapy section with supporting visuals
* Doula services section
* Community inclusion and engagement section
* Special thanks section
* Informative footer with quick links and contact details

## Design Approach

The website follows a clean, minimal, and professional visual style. Large imagery, soft backgrounds, rounded cards, and balanced typography help create a warm and trustworthy user experience.

The layout is designed to clearly communicate the organization's services, values, and commitment to supporting individuals and communities.
