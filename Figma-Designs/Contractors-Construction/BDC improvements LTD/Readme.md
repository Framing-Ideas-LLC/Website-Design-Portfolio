# BDC Improvements LTD

A professional construction and property improvement website designed for BDC Improvements LTD. The landing page presents the company’s maintenance, renovation, refurbishment, and property improvement services through a clean and structured layout.

## Features

* Full-width hero section with a clear construction-focused message and consultation CTA
* Company introduction and key value propositions
* Property improvement statistics and business highlights
* Services section covering extensions, refurbishments, general maintenance, and interior/exterior work
* Project showcase with completed construction and renovation work
* Step-by-step process section explaining how projects are handled
* Consultation and project inquiry section
* Construction news and blog section
* Customer testimonials
* Newsletter subscription section
* Responsive navigation, content sections, and footer
* Clean typography and professional construction-focused visual design
