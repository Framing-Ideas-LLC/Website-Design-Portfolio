# Facilities Management Services LLC

A modern and professional website designed for a cleaning and property maintenance company. The website presents the company's services, benefits, cleaning solutions, and safety standards in a clean and easy-to-navigate layout.

## Project Overview

The design focuses on building trust and clearly presenting professional cleaning and facility management services. It uses a clean white and light gray background with blue and green accents to create a professional and reliable appearance.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong headline and call-to-action button
* Company introduction and property maintenance information
* Service cards for different cleaning solutions
* Limited-time promotional banner
* Why choose us section with visual service highlights
* Before and after cleaning comparison
* Frequently asked questions section
* Health and safety information
* Final call-to-action banner
* Multi-column footer with navigation and contact details

## Services Highlighted

* Residential Pressure Washing
* Commercial Pressure Washing
* Gas Station Pressure Washing
* Carpet Cleaning
* Tile and Grout Cleaning
* Upholstery Cleaning

## Design Style

The website uses a modern corporate design with large imagery, rounded cards, clean typography, and spacious layouts. Blue is used as the primary brand color, while green accents help highlight service-related elements and important details.

## Purpose

The website is designed to help visitors quickly understand the company's cleaning and facilities management services, explore available solutions, and contact the business for professional assistance.
