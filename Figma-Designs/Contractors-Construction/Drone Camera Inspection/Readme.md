# Drone Camera Inspection

A modern and professional website designed for a drone photography, aerial inspection, and imaging service. The website focuses on showcasing drone capabilities, inspection services, pricing, photography work, and completed missions.

## Features

* Full-screen hero section with aerial drone imagery
* Clear navigation with Home, About Us, Our Features, and Gallery
* Service introduction section focused on aerial precision and drone inspection
* Service and pricing cards for different drone services
* Image gallery showcasing aerial photography
* Drone capabilities and service highlights
* Inspection showcase section with supporting imagery
* Drone performance statistics section
* Latest drone missions and project highlights
* Newsletter subscription section
* Contact details and footer navigation
* Clean responsive layout with strong visual presentation

## Sections

* Hero
* About Us
* Services and Pricing
* Gallery
* Drone Features
* Inspection Showcase
* Drone Statistics
* Latest Missions
* Newsletter
* Footer

## Design

The website uses a clean, modern layout with large drone photography, dark image-based sections, soft pink accent colors, and strong typography. The combination of aerial imagery and structured content creates a professional presentation for a drone inspection and photography business.
