# Iron Condor Club

A modern and professional trading education website designed for users who want to learn, understand, and apply stock market trading strategies.

The website uses a dark, premium visual style with financial charts, trading dashboards, market graphs, and clean typography to create a strong connection with the world of investing and stock trading.

## Main Features

* Modern dark-themed trading website
* Premium and professional financial visual design
* Hero section focused on proven trading techniques
* Trading charts and market data visuals
* Market gains and strategy-focused content sections
* Circular navigation cards for key values and services
* Trading strategy and educational service sections
* Call-to-action sections for joining and starting trading
* Portfolio or work showcase section
* Team introduction and expert profiles
* Client testimonial section
* Large promotional banner with trading visuals
* Contact form and footer navigation
* Fully responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

## Design Style

The design follows a clean, minimal, and premium approach. A dark background combined with subtle blue tones, thin borders, financial charts, and structured layouts creates a professional trading-focused experience.

The use of large typography, geometric shapes, circular elements, and market visualizations gives the website a modern and distinctive identity.
