## Da'Jerk Machine – Jamaican Restaurant Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Da'Jerk Machine** is a modern restaurant website concept designed in **Figma** to celebrate authentic Jamaican cuisine, family tradition, and the vibrant culture behind every dish. Led by Chef Sal and partner Candice, the brand brings together generations of culinary heritage, bold island flavors, and a passion for creating memorable experiences around food and community.

The website is crafted to showcase traditional Jamaican dishes, signature specialties, the restaurant's story, catering options, events, and dining experience while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Rich food photography, vibrant layouts, cultural storytelling, and intuitive navigation create an immersive journey that brings the warmth, flavors, and spirit of Jamaica to the digital experience.
