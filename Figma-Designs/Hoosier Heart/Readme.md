# Hoosier Heart

Hoosier Heart is a professional senior home care website designed to present compassionate and reliable care services for elderly individuals and their families. The website uses a warm, welcoming visual style with clear service information, strong calls to action, and trust-focused content.

## Key Features

* Responsive and user-friendly website layout
* Senior home care service presentation
* Bathing and toileting assistance section
* CPR classes and drug testing services
* Professional nursing care information
* Image gallery showcasing care and support
* Care statistics and trust indicators
* Multiple service and appointment call-to-actions
* Testimonials and client feedback section
* Career and professional hiring information
* Newsletter subscription form
* Contact information and footer navigation
* Clean and accessible navigation structure

## Design

The website follows a warm and caring design direction using cream, white, orange, red, and dark tones. Large lifestyle photography helps create an emotional connection, while cards and structured content sections make the services easy to understand. Typography is bold and modern, with clear visual hierarchy throughout the page.

## Purpose

The main purpose of Hoosier Heart is to help seniors and their families discover dependable home care services, understand available care options, schedule services, and connect with professional caregivers.
