## Business Showers – Empowering Entrepreneurs

Business Showers is a modern nonprofit and business-support website designed to empower entrepreneurs through professional training, mentorship, funding opportunities, and community-driven programs.

The website uses a bold and energetic visual style with vibrant pink and green branding, clean typography, strong imagery, and a structured layout that clearly communicates the organization’s mission and impact.

### Key Features

* Professional business training focused hero section
* Clear organization purpose and mission
* Community and event-focused imagery
* Impact statistics and achievements
* Business support and funding information
* Clean and responsive page structure
* Donation call-to-action
* Simple navigation for programs, sponsors, and organization information
* Contact details and social media links
* Consistent brand colors and typography

### Design Style

The design combines a professional nonprofit layout with an energetic community-focused identity. Bright pink accents create strong visual emphasis, while black, white, and soft pink backgrounds maintain readability and clear content separation.

### Purpose

The website is designed to showcase the impact of Business Showers while encouraging entrepreneurs to access training, mentorship, resources, and funding opportunities. It also provides a clear platform for supporters and sponsors to learn about the organization and contribute to its mission.
