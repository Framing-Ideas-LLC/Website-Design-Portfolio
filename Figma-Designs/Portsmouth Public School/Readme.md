## Portsmouth Public School Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Portsmouth Public School** is a modern educational website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase academic excellence, global learning, and student development through a professional and engaging digital experience. The design focuses on connecting students, parents, educators, and the wider community while reflecting the school's commitment to preparing learners for success in an increasingly interconnected world.

The website is crafted to highlight academic programs, admissions, student life, faculty, events, and school achievements while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Clean layouts, inspiring visuals, intuitive navigation, and a strong educational identity create an engaging journey that supports both current and prospective families.
