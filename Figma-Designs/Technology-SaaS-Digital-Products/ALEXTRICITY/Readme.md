## ALEXTRICITY – Electrical Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**ALEXTRICITY** is a modern electrical services website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase reliable, professional, and efficient electrical solutions through a clean and trustworthy digital experience. The design focuses on helping homeowners and businesses quickly understand available services, build confidence in the company, and easily request professional electrical assistance.

The website is crafted to highlight electrical services, service areas, expertise, emergency support, customer reviews, and contact options while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Strong layouts, clear typography, professional visuals, and intuitive navigation create an engaging journey that communicates reliability, safety, and technical expertise.
