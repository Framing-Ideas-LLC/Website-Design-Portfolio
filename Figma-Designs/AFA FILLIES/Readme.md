## AFA Fillies – Girls Boutique Soccer Club Website (Figma UI/UX)

**AFA Fillies** is a modern boutique soccer club website concept designed in **Figma** to inspire and empower young female athletes through competitive soccer, player development, and a strong sense of community. The design focuses on creating an energetic and engaging digital experience for players, parents, coaches, and supporters while showcasing the club's identity and commitment to developing girls both on and off the field.

The website is crafted to highlight teams, player development programs, training opportunities, schedules, tournaments, registration, club news, and community involvement while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Bold sports visuals, dynamic layouts, strong typography, and intuitive navigation create an exciting journey that reflects the energy and ambition of the AFA Fillies soccer community.
