# Elevated Operations – Construction & Restoration Website

## Overview

Elevated Operations is a modern business website designed for a construction, restoration, and property maintenance company. The design focuses on showcasing company services, completed projects, business experience, and customer trust while encouraging visitors to request a quote.

## Features

* Responsive and modern homepage design
* Full-width hero banner with call-to-action
* About company section
* Services showcase
* Featured solutions section
* Company statistics and experience highlights
* Recent projects section
* Progress and performance indicators
* Contact call-to-action section
* Newsletter subscription form
* Multi-column footer with business information

## Sections

1. Header with navigation and quote button
2. Hero banner
3. About the company
4. Services showcase
5. Why choose us
6. Features section
7. Promotional banner
8. Recent projects
9. Company expertise and performance
10. Contact CTA
11. Footer

## Design Style

* Clean and professional layout
* Orange, blue, white, and dark gray color palette
* Large service images
* Modern typography
* Card-based content sections
* Spacious and easy-to-read layout

## Technologies

* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript
* Responsive Design
