# Premium Luxury Car Rental Website

A modern and responsive luxury car rental website designed to provide a seamless vehicle browsing and reservation experience.

## Features

- Premium dark-themed UI with modern typography and gradient accents.
- Hero section with an integrated car reservation form.
- Vehicle collection with category filters.
- Luxury car listings with images, pricing, and specifications.
- Dedicated vehicle categories for Sport, Luxury SUV, Executive, and Supercar.
- Simple step-by-step booking process.
- Transparent pricing and rental information.
- Frequently Asked Questions section.
- Contact form for customer inquiries.
- Strong call-to-action sections for easy reservations.
- Responsive footer with navigation and contact details.

## Design

- Modern and premium automotive aesthetic.
- Dark background with neon accent colors.
- Clean layouts and structured content sections.
- High-quality vehicle imagery.
- User-friendly navigation and booking experience.
