# Online Realty Professionals

A modern real estate website designed for Online Realty Professionals, focused on helping buyers and sellers navigate real estate transactions.

## Overview

The website presents the company, its real estate services, team members, and available property search options through a clean and professional layout.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong homeownership-focused headline and call-to-action
* Property search section with location, price, property type, beds, and baths filters
* About Online Realty Professionals section
* Chicago real estate company introduction
* Meet the Team section with broker profiles and contact details
* Instagram property gallery
* Footer with navigation and company contact information

## Design

* Clean and professional real estate aesthetic
* Blue and white color palette
* Large property and city photography
* Serif typography for prominent headings
* Rounded cards, buttons, and image corners
* Spacious section layouts for improved readability
* Responsive structure suitable for desktop and mobile devices
