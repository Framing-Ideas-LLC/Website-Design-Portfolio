# Mozae Natural Mink Hair

A modern and elegant eCommerce website designed for a premium natural hair and beauty brand. The website focuses on showcasing high-quality virgin hair products through a clean layout, soft neutral color palette, and visually engaging imagery.

## Overview

Mozae Natural Mink Hair offers a luxury online shopping experience for customers looking for natural hair extensions, wigs, closures, frontals, and custom installations. The design combines a minimal aesthetic with warm tones to create a sophisticated and premium brand identity.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with promotional messaging and featured hair imagery
* Brand introduction highlighting quality, durability, and natural-looking hair
* Why Choose Us section showcasing key product benefits
* Story section focused on empowering women and celebrating natural beauty
* Product category showcase for bundles, wigs, closures, frontals, and custom installs
* Commitment section presenting the brand's quality and customer-focused values
* Frequently Asked Questions section
* Image gallery and newsletter signup
* Complete footer with navigation and additional information

## Design Approach

The website uses a clean editorial layout with generous spacing, elegant typography, soft beige and cream backgrounds, and gold accent colors. High-quality lifestyle photography is used throughout the website to create a premium and trustworthy shopping experience.
