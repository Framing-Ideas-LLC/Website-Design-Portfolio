## Too Sweet To Eat – Dessert Candle & Wax Melt E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Too Sweet To Eat** is a visually engaging e-commerce website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase handcrafted dessert candles and wax melts that combine realistic designs with inviting fragrances. The brand transforms the familiar beauty of desserts into unique home fragrance products, creating a delightful sensory experience for customers.

The website is crafted to highlight dessert-inspired candles, wax melts, featured collections, product details, brand story, and customer favorites while delivering a seamless shopping experience across all devices. Playful visuals, appetizing product photography, elegant layouts, and intuitive navigation create an engaging journey that makes every product feel as delightful as the real dessert it represents.
