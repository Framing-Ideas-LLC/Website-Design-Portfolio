# Ollie Mae’s Home Care Website

A modern and compassionate home care website designed to present Ollie Mae’s services in a clear, trustworthy, and approachable way. The website uses a clean layout, soft purple accents, and warm imagery to create a welcoming experience for seniors, families, and caregivers.

## Overview

The website introduces Ollie Mae’s Home Care and highlights its commitment to providing reliable, personalized, and compassionate care. The design focuses on building trust while making important information easy to find.

## Key Sections

### Hero Section

A welcoming introduction with a strong headline, supporting text, call-to-action button, and lifestyle imagery that reflects companionship and quality care.

### Care Services

A structured service section highlighting:

* Respite Care
* Transportation
* Companion Care
* Sitting Services
* Meal Preparation
* Medication Assistance

### About Section

A visual introduction to the company’s story, mission, and approach to compassionate home care.

### Call to Action

A dedicated section encouraging visitors to start their home care journey and get in touch with the team.

### Frequently Asked Questions

An accordion-style FAQ section designed to answer common questions about services, eligibility, service areas, and getting started.

### Blog Section

A collection of helpful articles focused on senior care, family support, daily routines, and home assistance.

### Contact Section

A clear contact area featuring business details alongside a qualification form for potential clients.

### Footer

A structured footer containing navigation links, additional resources, contact information, and copyright details.

## Design Approach

The design combines a soft and professional color palette with clean typography and rounded content cards. Large lifestyle images add warmth and emotional connection, while the spacious layout keeps the content easy to read and navigate.

## Goal

The goal of the website is to create a trustworthy digital presence that helps families easily understand available home care services, learn about the company, access helpful resources, and take the next step toward receiving care.
