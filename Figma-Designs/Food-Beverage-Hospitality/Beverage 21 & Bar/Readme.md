## Beverage 21 Bar & Grill – Restaurant & Bar Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Beverage 21 Bar & Grill** is a modern restaurant and bar website concept designed in **Figma** to capture its unique blend of dining, drinks, entertainment, and laid-back Baltimore vibes. Positioned as a destination that is "Not like the Others," the design focuses on creating an energetic and welcoming digital experience that reflects the personality of the venue.

The website is crafted to highlight the food menu, beverage offerings, atmosphere, events, entertainment, location, and guest experience while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Bold visuals, vibrant layouts, engaging typography, and intuitive navigation create an immersive journey that gives visitors a taste of the Beverage 21 experience before they arrive.



### Project Highlights

* Modern Bar & Grill UI/UX Design
* Bold & Entertainment-Focused Brand Experience
* Fully Responsive Layout
* Food & Dining Menu Showcase
* Beverage & Drinks Selection
* Restaurant Atmosphere & Gallery
* Events & Entertainment Sections
* Special Offers & Featured Experiences
* Baltimore Location & Community Focus
* Reservations & Contact Experience
* Customer Reviews & Social Proof
* Social Media & Community Engagement
* High-Fidelity Figma Prototype
* SEO & Performance-Oriented Structure
* Development-Ready Design System

This concept demonstrates how bold visual design, engaging storytelling, and intuitive navigation can help Beverage 21 Bar & Grill stand out in a competitive dining scene, communicate its unique personality, and create an exciting digital experience that encourages guests to visit, dine, and enjoy the atmosphere.

**Tools:** Figma • UI/UX Design • Auto Layout • Design System • Interactive Prototype • Responsive Web Design
