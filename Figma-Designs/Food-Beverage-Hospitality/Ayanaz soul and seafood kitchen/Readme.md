# Ayanaz Soul and Seafood Kitchen

Ayanaz Soul and Seafood Kitchen is a vibrant restaurant website designed to showcase the brand, signature dishes, customer favorites, and dining experience.

## Overview

The website uses a bold yellow and magenta color palette with food-focused imagery and handwritten typography accents. The layout combines modern restaurant branding with a warm, energetic visual style.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with restaurant branding, food imagery, headline, and menu CTA
* Brand story section introducing Ayanaz and its journey
* Fan Favorites section highlighting popular dishes
* Food gallery showcasing signature meals and drinks
* Menu section featuring selected food items with prices
* Cooking and preparation section highlighting fresh food and bold flavors
* Customer testimonials and reviews
* Contact section with inquiry form and business information
* Footer with navigation, privacy links, social links, and copyright information

## Design Style

* Bold yellow and magenta color scheme
* Large food photography
* Handwritten script typography combined with bold display fonts
* Rounded cards and image layouts
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Warm, playful, and energetic restaurant aesthetic
* Responsive layout suitable for desktop and mobile devices



## Purpose

The website is designed to build the Ayanaz brand, showcase its food and story, encourage visitors to explore the menu, and make it easy for customers to get in touch or visit the restaurant.
