# Sweets Website

A visually rich bakery and dessert website designed to showcase handcrafted sweets, cakes, cookies, and specialty desserts. The design combines a soft, elegant aesthetic with high-quality food photography and a clean editorial layout.

## Key Features

* Full-width hero section with featured dessert imagery and promotional messaging
* Product category cards for different sweets and dessert collections
* Featured products section with individual product images and names
* About section highlighting the brand's focus on quality and taste
* Dessert and cake showcase sections for different occasions
* Product filtering/navigation for dessert categories
* Promotional banners for special offers and products
* Kitchen-to-customer section showcasing featured items
* Newsletter/contact section with a simple inquiry form
* Instagram gallery for social media promotion
* Clean footer with navigation, company information, and important links

## Design Style

* Soft and elegant bakery-inspired visual style
* Warm neutral backgrounds with subtle pastel accents
* Large, high-quality product photography
* Minimal typography with decorative heading styles
* Spacious layouts with strong visual hierarchy
* Mix of centered content and editorial-style image/text sections
* Premium presentation focused heavily on food imagery
