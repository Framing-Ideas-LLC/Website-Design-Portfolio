# Smoothie Aid

Smoothie Aid is a clean and modern website design for a healthy beverage and nutrition brand. The design focuses on organic products, wellness, and a simple shopping experience.

## Features

* Clean and modern homepage layout
* Hero section with featured product and clear CTA
* Organic and nutrition-focused brand messaging
* Product benefits and key features section
* Step-by-step ordering process
* Featured products section with product cards
* Fresh order and delivery promotion section
* Guided care and wellness content
* Newsletter subscription section
* Simple footer with navigation and contact information
* Responsive layout designed for different screen sizes

## Design Style

The design uses a fresh and natural visual style with soft backgrounds, organic shapes, product photography, and bold typography. The color palette combines deep navy blue with light neutral tones and bright yellow/orange accents to create a healthy and energetic feel.
