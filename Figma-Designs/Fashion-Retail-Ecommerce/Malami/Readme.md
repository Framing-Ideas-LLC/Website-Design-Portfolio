# MALAMI – Modern Sleepwear & Loungewear Website

MALAMI is a modern eCommerce website designed for a stylish sleepwear and loungewear brand. The website focuses on creating a clean, elegant, and comfortable shopping experience while showcasing premium pajama collections for women and families.

## Homepage Overview

The homepage begins with a full-width hero section featuring a lifestyle image and the main message, “Discover The Art Of Modern Elevated Comfort.” A clear call-to-action encourages visitors to explore the latest collection.

The design then introduces the brand's best-selling products through a clean product grid, allowing users to quickly browse featured pajama sets and discover popular items.

A dedicated seasonal collection section highlights the Holiday 2025 Family Pajama Collection. This section uses a large lifestyle image and category cards to promote different collections, including family sets, silk and luxe styles, and morning or sleepwear collections.

The About MALAMI section introduces the brand and its focus on comfortable, stylish, and thoughtfully designed pajamas. A supporting lifestyle image creates a stronger connection between the brand story and its products.

The homepage also includes a “More Pajamas You'll Love” section that combines a large featured product image with smaller product cards, creating a visually balanced shopping experience.

Customer testimonials are displayed to build trust and provide social proof. The MALAMI Journal section shares additional content and stories related to sleep, comfort, lifestyle, and pajamas.

The website concludes with a promotional feature section and a detailed footer containing navigation links, customer information, social links, and newsletter signup functionality.

## Key Features

* Modern and elegant fashion-focused design
* Full-width lifestyle hero section
* Best-selling product showcase
* Seasonal and family pajama collections
* Category-based shopping sections
* Brand story and About section
* Featured product grids
* Customer testimonials
* Lifestyle and journal content
* Newsletter subscription
* Complete eCommerce navigation and footer

## Design Style

The overall design uses a soft, minimal, and sophisticated visual style. Neutral backgrounds, large lifestyle photography, clean typography, and spacious layouts create a premium shopping experience. The combination of editorial-style imagery and structured product grids helps the brand feel both fashionable and approachable.

## Goal

The main goal of the MALAMI website is to create an engaging online shopping experience that presents sleepwear as both comfortable and stylish. The design helps users easily explore collections, discover products, connect with the brand story, and confidently move toward making a purchase.
