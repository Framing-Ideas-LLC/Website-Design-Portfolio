## Potters Klay – Home Health Care Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Potters Klay** is a modern home health care website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase compassionate, personalized, and affordable care for seniors and individuals with disabilities. As a women-owned, family-operated organization with over 15 years of experience, the design focuses on building trust, communicating expertise, and making essential care information easy to access for clients and caregivers.

The website is crafted to highlight home health care services, the organization's mission, caregiver support, client resources, and key differentiators while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm visuals, accessible layouts, clear content hierarchy, and intuitive navigation create a welcoming journey for families seeking dependable care and support at home.

This concept demonstrates how thoughtful design, accessible information architecture, and compassionate storytelling can help a home health care provider build trust with families, clearly communicate its services, and create a supportive digital experience for both clients and caregivers.
