# Bootsy

Bootsy is a modern fashion e-commerce website focused on stylish boot covers and contemporary footwear collections. The website combines a bold editorial layout with high-quality fashion imagery to create a strong and engaging shopping experience.

## Features

* Modern fashion-focused homepage design
* Hero section with featured boot collection
* Featured collection and product sections
* Bestselling products showcase
* Limited edition collection section
* Customer testimonials
* Newsletter subscription section
* Responsive and visually engaging layout
* Strong typography and editorial-style presentation
* Product-focused call-to-action sections

## Pages

* Home
* Collection
* Product
* About Us
* Contact
* Newsletter

## Design

The website uses a dark, high-contrast visual style with bold typography, vibrant product imagery, pink accent colors, and large editorial sections. The layout is designed to give the brand a premium and fashion-forward appearance.

## Purpose

The goal of Bootsy is to present boot covers and footwear collections through a visually striking e-commerce experience that encourages users to explore collections, discover bestselling products, and make purchases.
