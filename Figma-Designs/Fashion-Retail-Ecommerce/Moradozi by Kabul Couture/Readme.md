# Moradozi by Kabul Couture

A modern and elegant e-commerce website designed for Moradozi by Kabul Couture, showcasing traditional Afghan fashion, handcrafted dresses, scarves, shawls, accessories, and cultural products.

The website combines a luxury visual style with a clean shopping experience, allowing users to explore different product categories and discover traditional Afghan craftsmanship.

## Features

* Modern luxury fashion design
* Full-width hero section with promotional content
* Featured product collections
* Traditional Afghan clothing and accessories
* Product category sections
* Promotional banners and featured collections
* Image-focused product cards
* Newsletter subscription section
* Customer testimonials
* Instagram gallery
* Responsive layout for desktop and mobile devices
* Clean footer with navigation and contact links

## Design Approach

The website uses a premium and cultural visual direction that highlights Afghan heritage through large imagery, traditional patterns, elegant typography, and spacious layouts. The overall experience focuses on presenting products in a modern way while maintaining the identity and craftsmanship of Kabul Couture.
