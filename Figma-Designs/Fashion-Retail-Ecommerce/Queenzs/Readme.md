# Queenz Like Me

A modern and inspiring nonprofit website designed to empower young girls through mentorship, leadership development, workshops, and community programs.

## Overview

The website introduces Queenz Like Me and its mission to help young girls build confidence, develop leadership skills, and grow through meaningful mentorship and personal development opportunities.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong mission statement and clear call-to-action buttons
* Impact statistics highlighting girls mentored, workshops, events, and volunteers
* About section introducing the organization and its purpose
* Benefits section covering confidence building, leadership skills, personal growth, and positive mentorship
* Full-width call-to-action section encouraging visitors to join the community
* Testimonials sharing experiences and success stories
* Footer with navigation links, contact information, and social media links

## Design Style

The design uses a clean and modern layout with a soft purple color palette. Large editorial-style typography creates an inspiring and empowering visual identity, while spacious layouts, rounded cards, and authentic imagery give the website a warm and community-focused feel.


## Purpose

The website is designed to connect young girls, mentors, volunteers, and supporters with the Queenz Like Me community while clearly communicating the organization's mission, impact, and opportunities to get involved.
