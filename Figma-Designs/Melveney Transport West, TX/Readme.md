# Melveney Transport Website

## Project Description

Melveney Transport is a modern and professional website designed for a truck parts and transportation business. The website focuses on helping customers easily explore reliable truck parts, browse products, and get in touch with the company.

The design uses a clean layout with a strong combination of dark backgrounds, white sections, and bright lime green accents. The overall visual style creates a professional and industrial look that matches the trucking and heavy-duty parts industry.

## Key Sections

### Hero Section

The website starts with a large hero banner featuring a truck image and a clear headline:

**Drive Strong With Affordable Truck Parts**

The hero section also includes a short description and a call-to-action button that encourages users to explore available parts.

### Features Section

A small feature section highlights the main benefits of the business:

* Heavy-Duty Strength
* Tough Performance
* Built For Endurance

### About and Introduction Section

This section introduces the company and its products with supporting images from the transportation and truck parts industry. It focuses on providing reliable and long-lasting truck parts.

### Product Categories

The website allows users to explore different truck part categories, including:

* Fleet
* Electronics
* Brake Parts
* Exhausts

Each category is presented using large visual cards to make browsing easier.

### Featured Products

The website includes product sliders showcasing different truck parts. Each product card contains:

* Product image
* Product name
* Rating
* Price

This section helps customers quickly discover available products.

### Statistics Section

A highlighted section displays key business numbers such as:

* 15K+ Customers
* 99% Customer Satisfaction
* 30+ Countries Served
* 20K+ Products

### Why Choose Us

This section explains the main advantages of choosing Melveney Transport and includes supporting visuals and product-related content.

### Contact Section

The website includes company contact information along with a simple contact form where users can send messages directly to the business.

### Customer Testimonials

A testimonial section displays customer feedback to build trust and credibility.

### Footer

The footer includes:

* Company information
* Quick links
* Shop links
* Contact details
* Newsletter subscription

## Design Style

The website follows a modern industrial e-commerce design approach. The main design characteristics include:

* Clean and structured layout
* Dark and light section contrast
* Lime green accent color
* Large transportation and truck-related imagery
* Product-focused content
* Card-based layouts
* Responsive design
* Clear call-to-action buttons
