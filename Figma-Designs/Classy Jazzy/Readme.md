## Classy Jazzy – Beauty E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Classy Jazzy** is a modern beauty e-commerce website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase inclusive, accessible, and high-quality beauty products through a vibrant and engaging online shopping experience. Founded in 2019, the brand was created to address the lack of quality and inclusivity within the beauty industry, offering products designed to make everyone feel confident and represented.

The website is crafted to highlight lip gloss, body butter, skincare, beauty collections, featured products, and the brand's story while delivering a seamless shopping experience across all devices. Bold visuals, expressive layouts, intuitive navigation, and a strong product-focused design create an engaging journey from discovery to checkout.
