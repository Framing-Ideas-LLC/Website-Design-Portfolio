# Mind Your Music

Mind Your Music is a modern entertainment website designed to promote interactive live music experiences. The website combines vibrant visuals, dark-themed styling, and engaging content to showcase music games, live performances, and audience participation.

## Features

* Responsive and modern website design
* Full-width hero section with a live concert background
* Interactive music experience cards
* Live Music Jukebox section
* Open Mic feature
* Live Music Bingo feature
* Games and challenges showcase
* Image-based content sections
* About Us section
* Call-to-action sections
* Newsletter subscription form
* Navigation and social media links
* Mobile-friendly layout

## Design

The website uses a dark purple background with neon-inspired blue and pink accents. Large imagery, concert visuals, and entertainment-focused graphics create an immersive and energetic experience.



## Purpose

The project is designed to create an engaging online presence for Mind Your Music and encourage users to explore live music, games, challenges, and interactive entertainment experiences.
