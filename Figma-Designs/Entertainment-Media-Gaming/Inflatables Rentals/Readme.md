# Joy Inflatables

A colorful and family-friendly website for Joy Inflatables, a bounce house and party rental company serving customers in the Florida area.

## Overview

The website is designed to showcase inflatable rentals, combo packages, party extras, and special offers while making it easy for customers to browse products and submit rental reservations.

## Key Features

* Hero section with a strong rental-focused headline and booking CTA
* Category navigation for Bouncers, Combos, and Slides
* Online reservation section
* Specials and discounts section
* Company introduction and service overview
* Combo rental product cards with pricing and booking buttons
* Party extras including popcorn, snow cone, and cotton candy machines
* Delivery area information
* Dedicated section for churches and schools
* Contact form for customer inquiries
* Simple navigation and footer menu

## Design

The design uses a bright, playful, and family-oriented visual style that fits the party rental industry. Colorful backgrounds, rounded cards, large product imagery, and bold typography create an energetic experience while keeping the layout easy to navigate.
