# Cadre Dolo

Cadre Dolo is a dark, bold, and modern music artist website designed around the artist’s identity, music, story, and lifestyle. The website combines a cinematic visual style with strong typography, gold accents, and immersive imagery to create a premium music brand experience.

## Features

* Modern dark-themed design
* Bold typography with gold accent colors
* Responsive hero section with artist imagery
* Latest music and release cards
* Artist story and biography section
* Music collection and featured releases
* Merchandise showcase
* Lifestyle and brand-focused visual sections
* Social media and movement section
* Contact and newsletter signup area
* Responsive layout for different screen sizes

## Sections

* Hero
* Latest Releases
* The Story
* Music Collection
* Merchandise
* Brand Showcase
* Social Media
* Contact
* Footer

## Design

The design uses a black background, warm gold highlights, high-contrast typography, and large photography to create a bold and premium visual identity. The layout focuses on music, storytelling, and personal branding while keeping the overall interface clean and structured.



## Purpose

The website is designed to showcase Cadre Dolo’s music, story, merchandise, and overall brand while providing visitors with a strong visual experience and easy access to the artist’s latest work.
