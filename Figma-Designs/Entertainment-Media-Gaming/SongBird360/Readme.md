# SongBird 360 Photo Booth Website

## Overview

SongBird 360 is a modern event photo booth website designed to promote photo booth experiences, showcase available packages, and encourage visitors to book an event.

## Key Features

* Hero section with a strong headline, event photography, and booking CTAs
* Signature Photo Booth Experiences section
* Service cards for 360 Photo Booth, Roaming Photo Booth, and Digital Photo Booth
* Highlight section explaining the company's event experience and services
* Pricing section with multiple photo booth packages
* About SongBird 360 section with company information and customer-focused messaging
* Full-width CTA section for event bookings
* Contact section with business information and booking form
* Simple footer with navigation and contact details

## Design

* Elegant and modern visual style
* Cream and dark green base colors with teal and pink gradient accents
* Serif typography combined with italic accent text for a premium editorial look
* Large photography used throughout the layout to showcase real event experiences
* Card-based service and pricing layouts for easy content scanning
* Clear spacing and section separation for a smooth browsing experience
* Responsive structure designed for desktop and mobile layouts
