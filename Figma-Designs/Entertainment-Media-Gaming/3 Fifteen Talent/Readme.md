## 3 Fifteen Talent Solutions – Recruitment Website (Figma UI/UX)

**3 Fifteen Talent Solutions, LLC** is a modern recruitment and talent solutions website concept designed in **Figma** to connect businesses with exceptional talent through a professional, efficient, and people-focused digital experience. The design emphasizes trust, expertise, and innovation while positioning the company as a strategic partner for organizations seeking to build high-performing teams.

The website is crafted to showcase full-cycle recruiting, targeted advertising, strategic sourcing, interview coaching, process improvement, and talent consulting services while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Clean layouts, confident typography, intuitive navigation, and compelling content create an engaging journey for both employers and candidates.
