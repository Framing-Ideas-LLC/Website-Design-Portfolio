## Hard Knox Epoxy LLC – High-Performance Epoxy Flooring Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Hard Knox Epoxy LLC** is a professional flooring website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase high-performance epoxy flooring systems engineered for durability, strength, and long-term performance. The design positions the company as a skilled flooring specialist that combines proper surface preparation, premium resin systems, industrial-grade materials, and meticulous installation practices.

The website is crafted to highlight residential and commercial epoxy flooring, flooring systems, installation processes, project applications, material quality, and workmanship guarantees while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Bold industrial imagery, strong typography, structured layouts, and rugged visual details create a confident digital experience that communicates craftsmanship, reliability, and performance.
