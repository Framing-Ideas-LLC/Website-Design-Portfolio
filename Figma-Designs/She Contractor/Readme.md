## She Contractor – Women in Construction Community Website (Figma UI/UX)

**She Contractor** is a modern community and educational website concept designed in **Figma** to empower women in the construction industry through education, networking, mentorship, and professional development. The design focuses on inspiring growth, increasing visibility, and creating opportunities for women to advance their careers within the building trades.

The website is crafted to showcase training programs, educational seminars, industry resources, networking events, success stories, and community initiatives while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Bold visuals, modern layouts, intuitive navigation, and engaging storytelling create an inspiring digital platform that supports tradeswomen at every stage of their professional journey.
