# Ntonji Zanzibar Tours & Safaris

A modern and visually engaging tourism website designed for Ntonji Zanzibar Tours & Safaris. The website focuses on showcasing Zanzibar travel experiences, tour packages, attractions, and travel services through a clean and image-focused layout.

## Key Features

* Full-width hero section with destination imagery and strong call-to-action
* Ntonji Zanzibar Tours & Safaris introduction section
* Featured Zanzibar tour packages with pricing and booking options
* Tour package cards with destination imagery and short descriptions
* Frequently Asked Questions section with expandable questions
* Newsletter subscription section
* Navigation with links to Home, About Us, Tours, Attractions, Blog, and Contact Us
* Contact information and quick links in the footer
* Travel-focused visual design using Zanzibar landscapes and destination photography
* Clear typography and structured content hierarchy
* Responsive layout designed for different screen sizes

## Design

The design uses a travel-inspired visual style with vibrant destination photography, warm orange accents, deep blue tones, rounded cards, and clean white sections. Large imagery is used throughout the page to create an immersive tourism experience while keeping the content easy to scan.
