# Penn Tag Services

A professional and modern website design for Penn Tag Services, focused on notary, PennDOT tag, title, and related document services.

## Overview

The website provides a clean and trustworthy digital experience for customers looking for reliable notary and vehicle-related services. The design combines a professional layout with strong typography, structured content sections, and service-focused visuals.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a clear service-focused headline and appointment CTA
* Comprehensive notary services section
* Service categories for Bill of Sale, Trusts, Living Trusts, and Power of Attorney
* Company introduction and service statistics
* EZPass GoPak service section with pricing details
* Company achievements and mission-focused content
* Customer testimonials and reviews
* Appointment booking section
* Newsletter signup
* Footer with company information, quick links, and service details

## Design

The website uses a professional navy, white, and dark color palette to create a trustworthy and established brand presence. Large serif headings are combined with clean supporting typography, while photography is used throughout the layout to reinforce the legal and document-service theme.
