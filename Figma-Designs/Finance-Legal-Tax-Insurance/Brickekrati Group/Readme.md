# Brickekrati Group – Business Consulting Website

## Project Overview

Brickekrati Group is a modern corporate business consulting website designed to showcase the company's expertise, services, achievements, and client success stories. The website features a professional layout with a strong visual hierarchy, making it easy for visitors to learn about the company and get in touch for business consultation.

## Design Style

* Clean and modern corporate design
* Professional business-focused layout
* Minimal UI with spacious sections
* Strong typography and balanced spacing
* High-quality business photography
* Clear call-to-action buttons throughout the website

## Color Palette

* White
* Black
* Dark Gray
* Bright Pink (Primary Accent)

## Typography

* Bold headings for strong visual impact
* Simple sans-serif font for body content
* Clear content hierarchy for better readability
