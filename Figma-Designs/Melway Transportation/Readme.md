## Melway Transportation – Vehicle Transport & Auto Hauling Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Melway Transportation** is a modern vehicle transportation website concept designed in **Figma** to provide a trustworthy, convenient, and stress-free digital experience for customers looking to transport their vehicles. As a family-owned and operated car hauling brokerage based in Florida, the design focuses on communicating reliability, safety, affordability, and personalized service across nationwide routes.

The website is crafted to highlight vehicle transportation services, nationwide coverage, insured hauling solutions, transport options, the company's family-owned story, and quote requests while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Strong automotive imagery, clean layouts, bold typography, and intuitive navigation create a professional digital experience that makes vehicle shipping simple and transparent.
