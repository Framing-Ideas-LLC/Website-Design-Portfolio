# Melway Transportation

A modern and professional transportation website designed for a car shipping and vehicle logistics company. The website focuses on building trust, showcasing transportation services, and making it easy for customers to request a quote.

## Overview

The design features a bold and structured layout with a dark theme, strong red accents, and high-quality vehicle imagery. Large typography, clear call-to-action sections, and visual statistics help communicate the company's experience and reliability.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong headline and vehicle transportation imagery
* Company statistics highlighting experience, customers, and completed shipments
* About section introducing Melway Transportation
* Vehicle transportation services
* Featured vehicle showcase sections
* Why choose us section with key benefits
* Vehicle gallery displaying recent transportation work
* Customer testimonials
* Latest transportation news and blog articles
* Contact and call-to-action sections
* Multi-column footer with navigation and company information

## Design Style

The website uses a bold visual identity built around black, white, and red. Large vehicle photography creates a strong connection to the transportation industry, while clean layouts and structured content blocks keep the experience professional and easy to navigate.
