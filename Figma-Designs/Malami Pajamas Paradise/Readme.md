## Malami Pajamas Paradise – Luxury Sleepwear E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Malami Pajamas Paradise** is a soft and elegant sleepwear e-commerce website concept designed in **Figma** to create a calming digital shopping experience inspired by slow nights, peaceful mornings, and everyday luxury. The design reflects the brand's focus on comfortable sleepwear made with care, soothing colors, and thoughtful details.

The website is crafted to highlight pajama collections, fabric quality, product details, seasonal styles, and comfortable sleepwear essentials while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Soft visual elements, calming imagery, refined typography, and spacious layouts create a relaxing experience that mirrors the comfort and tranquility of the products.
