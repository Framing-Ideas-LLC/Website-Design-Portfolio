## Miramar Landing – HOA Community Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Miramar Landing** is a modern community website concept designed in **Figma** to strengthen homeowner engagement, improve community communication, and support the long-term beauty and property values of the neighborhood. The design focuses on making it easier for residents to stay informed, participate in HOA initiatives, and contribute to the continued success of the community.

The website is crafted to highlight HOA information, community committees, announcements, neighborhood guidelines, maintenance updates, resident resources, and community involvement opportunities while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Clean layouts, organized content, intuitive navigation, and community-focused visuals create an accessible digital experience for homeowners and residents.
