# Juice Business Website

A vibrant and fresh juice business website designed to promote healthy fruit juices, showcase products, and build customer trust.

## Overview

The website uses a bright, energetic visual style with yellow, orange, cream, green, and red tones. The design focuses on freshness, natural ingredients, healthy living, and fruit-based products.

## Main Sections

* Hero section with a welcoming headline, short introduction, and Shop Now CTA
* About section introducing the juice business and its focus on health and wellness
* Featured Products section displaying popular juice bottles with pricing
* Brand story section introducing the owner and business journey
* Fresh Juice gallery showcasing products, ingredients, and brand photography
* Recommended Juices section featuring different juice flavors and products
* Why Choose Us section highlighting natural ingredients, no added sugars, preservative-free products, and health-focused juices
* Customer Testimonials section displaying client reviews
* Newsletter section with an email subscription form
* Footer with navigation links, company information, and contact details

## Design Style

* Bright and playful visual direction
* Fruit-inspired color palette
* Cream and yellow background sections
* Orange decorative elements and wave shapes
* Fresh fruit photography
* Rounded product images and cards
* Bold decorative typography for headings
* Clean and readable supporting text
* Strong visual separation between sections

## User Experience

The layout is designed to guide visitors from the brand introduction toward featured products and recommended juices. Clear CTA buttons encourage users to shop, learn about the brand, and explore the available juice options.

## Responsive Design

The website structure is suitable for desktop and mobile layouts, with content organized into clear sections that can adapt to smaller screen sizes.
