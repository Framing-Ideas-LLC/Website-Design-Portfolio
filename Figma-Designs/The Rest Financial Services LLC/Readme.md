# The Rest Financial Services LLC

A modern and professional financial services website designed to present tax, accounting, consulting, and credit-related services in a clear and trustworthy way. The website uses a clean layout, soft neutral colors, and pink accent elements to create a polished and approachable brand identity.

## Features

* Modern and responsive website design
* Professional hero section with clear call-to-action
* Tax and financial services overview
* Personal tax services
* Business tax services
* Notary services
* Credit repair solutions
* Company introduction and service highlights
* Statistics and success-focused section
* Financial consulting and advisory services
* Featured projects and case studies
* Expert guidance call-to-action section
* Financial advice and resources blog
* Newsletter subscription section
* Complete footer with services, contact details, and navigation

## Design Focus

The design focuses on professionalism, trust, and financial expertise. Large typography, structured content sections, professional imagery, and subtle pink accents create a clean and modern user experience while making important services and calls-to-action easy to access.
