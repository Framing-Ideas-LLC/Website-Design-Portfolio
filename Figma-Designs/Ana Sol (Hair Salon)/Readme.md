# Ana Sol Hair Salon Website

A premium and elegant hair salon website designed to showcase professional hair services, transformations, and the salon experience. The design uses a dark luxury aesthetic with warm golden accents, refined typography, and high-quality hair photography to create a sophisticated visual identity.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with salon introduction and appointment CTAs
* Navigation for Home, Services, Gallery, About, and Contact
* Statistics highlighting experience, clients, services, and ratings
* Services section featuring hair styling, color, treatments, and bridal services
* Before and after gallery showcasing different hairstyles and transformations
* Studio introduction with salon image and key benefits
* Client reviews and testimonials
* Final booking CTA section
* Footer with navigation, contact details, working hours, and social links

## Design Style

The website follows a modern luxury style with a dark background, golden highlights, elegant serif typography, and spacious layouts. The visual hierarchy focuses on showcasing hair transformations while making appointment booking easily accessible throughout the website.
