## GYM Lab – Performance Gym Wear E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**GYM Lab** is a modern e-commerce website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase performance-driven gym wear created for lifters, athletes, and individuals committed to continuous progress. The design focuses on building a bold, high-energy shopping experience that reflects strength, discipline, performance, and an active lifestyle.

The website is crafted to highlight gym apparel, training essentials, featured collections, new releases, and best-selling products while delivering a seamless shopping experience across all devices. Strong visuals, bold typography, athletic imagery, and streamlined navigation create an engaging journey from product discovery to checkout.
