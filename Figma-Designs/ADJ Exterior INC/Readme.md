# ADJ Exterior INC

A modern and professional roofing and exterior services website designed for ADJ Exterior INC. The website presents the company’s services, expertise, service areas, and inspection process through a clean layout with strong visuals and clear call-to-action sections.

## Project Overview

The website is focused on building trust and generating leads for residential and exterior improvement services. It uses a combination of construction imagery, bold typography, red accent colors, and structured content sections to create a reliable and professional brand presence.

## Key Sections

* Header with contact information, navigation links, and quote button
* Hero section with a strong headline and primary call-to-action
* About section introducing the company and its services
* Service showcase featuring roofing and exterior solutions
* Step-by-step inspection and project process
* Why choose us section highlighting experience and customer benefits
* Service area section featuring Sugar Grove and Fox Valley
* Free inspection call-to-action section
* Detailed footer with navigation, services, legal pages, and company information

## Design Style

The design follows a modern construction and home improvement aesthetic. A white and light gray background is combined with bold red accents and dark sections to create strong contrast. Large construction images help communicate the company’s expertise, while clean spacing and structured layouts improve readability.
