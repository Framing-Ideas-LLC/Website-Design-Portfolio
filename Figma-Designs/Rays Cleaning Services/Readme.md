# Ray's Cleaning Services

A modern and professional cleaning services website designed to showcase residential and commercial cleaning solutions with a clean, trustworthy, and user-friendly interface.

## Overview

This website presents Ray's Cleaning Services through a structured layout that highlights the company's services, experience, cleaning process, customer testimonials, frequently asked questions, and contact options.

## Features

* Modern and responsive website design
* Professional hero section with clear call-to-action buttons
* Service showcase section
* Company statistics and promotional video section
* Step-by-step cleaning process
* Customer testimonials
* Frequently asked questions
* Contact and booking form
* Responsive layout for different screen sizes

## Sections

* Home
* About
* Services
* Cleaning Solutions
* Cleaning Process
* Testimonials
* FAQ
* Contact
