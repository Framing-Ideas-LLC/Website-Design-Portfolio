# Travel & Tourism Website

A modern and visually engaging travel website designed to help users discover destinations, explore travel experiences, and plan memorable trips with ease. The website combines destination discovery, travel packages, activities, blogs, testimonials, and contact functionality within a clean and user-friendly layout.

## Overview

The website focuses on making travel planning simple and inspiring. Visitors can quickly search for destinations, select tour types and travel dates, explore popular locations, read travel stories, and discover different activities and experiences.

## Key Features

* Clean and modern travel-focused design
* Destination and tour search functionality
* Tour type and travel month selection
* Popular destination showcase
* Travel packages and destination cards
* About and traveler experience section
* Adventure travel section
* Activity and experience categories
* Travel blog and latest articles
* Customer testimonials
* Strong call-to-action sections
* Contact and inquiry form
* Newsletter subscription
* Complete footer navigation
* Responsive and mobile-friendly structure

## Design Style

The website uses a fresh and welcoming visual style with green and yellow accent colors, large travel photography, rounded cards, subtle backgrounds, and elegant typography. The combination of vibrant imagery and spacious layouts creates an adventurous yet professional travel experience.

## Main Sections

1. Header and Navigation
2. Hero and Travel Search
3. Top Values
4. About / Traveler Story
5. Popular Destinations
6. Adventure Travel
7. Travel Activities
8. Travel Blog
9. Call to Action
10. Customer Testimonials
11. Contact Form
12. Newsletter and Footer

## Purpose

The main purpose of this website is to provide travelers with an easy way to discover destinations, explore tours and activities, find travel inspiration, and contact the travel company for trip planning and bookings.
