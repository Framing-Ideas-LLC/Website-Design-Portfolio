## Purrfect Knotworks

A handcrafted e-commerce website designed for Purrfect Knotworks, featuring a warm, artisan-inspired visual style focused on handmade crochet and leather products.

### Key Features

* Full-width hero section with a strong product-focused visual
* Handmade product collection showcase
* Product cards with names and pricing
* About section highlighting the craftsmanship and brand story
* Shop by category section
* Product discovery section with category filters
* Seasonal promotional banners
* Crochet and handmade creation showcase
* Brand statistics and trust-building content
* Customer testimonial section
* Contact form for customer inquiries
* Newsletter and footer navigation
* Responsive and visually structured e-commerce layout

### Design

The design uses a dark, earthy background combined with warm beige and cream tones to create a handcrafted and premium feel. Large product imagery, textured backgrounds, and editorial-style sections reinforce the handmade nature of the brand.

The overall layout balances product-focused sections with storytelling content to create a more engaging shopping experience.
