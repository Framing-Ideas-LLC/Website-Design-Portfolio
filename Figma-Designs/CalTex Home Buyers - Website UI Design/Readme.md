# CalTex Home Buyers

A modern real estate website designed for CalTex Home Buyers, focused on helping homeowners sell their properties quickly and easily.

## Project Overview

The website provides a professional and user-friendly experience for homeowners looking to sell their properties. It highlights the company's services, selling process, benefits, customer testimonials, and frequently asked questions.

## Main Sections

- **Hero Section:** Introduces the property-selling service with a clear headline and call-to-action.
- **About Section:** Provides an introduction to the company and its approach.
- **Property Solutions:** Showcases different property types and available solutions.
- **How It Works:** Explains the three simple steps to get started.
- **Why Sell to Us:** Highlights the key benefits of selling through CalTex Home Buyers.
- **Before and After:** Shows property transformation examples.
- **Contact Form:** Allows homeowners to submit their property details for an inquiry.
- **Testimonials:** Displays customer feedback and experiences.
- **FAQ Section:** Answers common questions about the property-selling process.
- **Footer:** Includes company information, navigation links, and contact details.

## Design Overview

- Clean and modern real estate layout.
- Navy blue, white, and orange color palette.
- Professional typography and clear content hierarchy.
- Property-focused imagery.
- Consistent call-to-action buttons.
- Structured sections for easy navigation.
