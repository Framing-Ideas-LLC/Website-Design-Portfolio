# Psalms Of Hope

A modern and compassionate home care website designed for Psalms of Hope Companion Services LLC. The website focuses on trust, comfort, dignity, and personalized support for seniors and families.

## Project Overview

The homepage features a warm and professional visual style with calming colors, clean typography, and caregiving-focused imagery. The design clearly communicates the company's services, experience, service areas, and values.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with clear call-to-action buttons and company statistics
* About section introducing the home care services and healthcare experience
* Services section highlighting companionship, meal preparation, medication reminders, housekeeping, transportation, and personal care
* Service areas section covering different counties and surrounding locations
* Why choose us section focused on compassionate and dependable care
* Contact section with contact details and a service inquiry form
* Professional footer with services, service areas, and company information

## Design Approach

The interface uses a soft blue, white, and neutral color palette to create a calm and trustworthy healthcare experience. Large caregiving images, structured content cards, subtle gradients, and clean spacing help create a professional and approachable user experience.

## Goal

The goal of the website is to make it easy for families to understand available care services, build trust in the company, explore service areas, and quickly get in touch for personalized home care support.
