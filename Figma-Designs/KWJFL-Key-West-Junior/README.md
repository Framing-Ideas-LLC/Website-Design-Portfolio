## KWJFL – Key West Junior Football League Website (Figma UI/UX)

**KWJFL (Key West Junior Football League)** is a modern **parallax website concept** designed in **Figma** to inspire young athletes, engage families, and strengthen the league's online presence. The design captures the excitement of youth football through dynamic visuals, immersive scrolling, and a clean, user-focused interface.

The website is crafted to showcase league information, team highlights, schedules, registration, news, and community involvement while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Bold typography, energetic layouts, and interactive parallax effects create an engaging journey for players, parents, coaches, and supporters.

### Project Highlights

* Premium Sports UI/UX Design
* Modern Parallax Scrolling Experience
* Fully Responsive Layout
* Youth Football League Branding
* Team & Player Showcase
* Match Schedules & League Standings
* Online Registration & Volunteer Sections
* Interactive News & Events
* High-Fidelity Figma Prototype
* SEO & Performance-Oriented Structure
* Development-Ready Design System

This concept demonstrates how modern design, intuitive navigation, and engaging storytelling can help a youth football league build a stronger digital community, improve communication, and encourage participation both on and off the field.

**Tools:** Figma • UI/UX Design • Auto Layout • Design System • Interactive Prototype • Responsive Web Design
