## Markeida Faithe, LICSW – Therapy & Spiritual Guidance Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Markeida Faithe, LICSW** is a modern personal practice website concept designed in **Figma** to introduce a licensed clinical social worker, author, and spiritual guide dedicated to supporting individuals on their journey toward inner healing, personal growth, and spiritual freedom. The design brings together professional expertise and spiritual insight to create a warm, trustworthy, and reflective digital experience.

The website is crafted to highlight therapy and guidance services, professional credentials, author work, spiritual resources, personal philosophy, and opportunities to connect while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Calm imagery, thoughtful typography, soft layouts, and intuitive navigation create an inviting experience that reflects the brand's focus on holistic growth and meaningful transformation.
