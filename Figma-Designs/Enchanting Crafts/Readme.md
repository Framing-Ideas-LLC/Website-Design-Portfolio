# Enchanting Crafts

A handcrafted e-commerce website designed for a creative workshop selling unique, collectible art pieces.

## Overview

The website combines a warm editorial style with a modern e-commerce experience. It focuses on showcasing handmade products, collections, and the story behind the workshop while keeping the shopping experience simple and easy to navigate.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with featured product imagery and primary shopping CTA
* Collection section showcasing five product categories
* Product grid featuring popular and reordered items
* Workshop introduction with supporting product imagery
* Step-by-step section explaining the ordering and production process
* FAQ section covering common customer questions
* Footer with navigation, shop links, company information, and newsletter signup

## Design Style

* Warm cream and dark green color palette
* Editorial-inspired typography
* Large product photography
* Minimal and clean navigation
* Rounded product cards
* Subtle borders and spacing
* Handmade and premium visual aesthetic
