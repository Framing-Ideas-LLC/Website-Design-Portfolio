## Basketball Camp – Elite Basketball Training Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Basketball Camp** is a high-energy sports training website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase an elite basketball development experience built around professional coaching, advanced skill development, competitive training, and player growth. The design is focused on motivating athletes to challenge themselves, sharpen their abilities, and take their game to the next level.

The website is crafted to highlight camp programs, training sessions, coaching expertise, player development, competitive drills, schedules, registration information, and camp experiences while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Dynamic basketball photography, bold athletic typography, energetic layouts, and strong calls to action create an engaging digital experience designed to inspire athletes and their families.
