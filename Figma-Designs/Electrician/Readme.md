# Electrician Services Website

A modern and professional electrician services website designed to showcase residential and commercial electrical solutions. The website uses a dark theme with red accent colors to create a strong and high-contrast visual identity.

## Project Overview

The website is designed for an electrical service business, focusing on emergency services, electrical maintenance, installations, troubleshooting, circuit work, and high-voltage solutions.

## Main Sections

* Hero section with a strong headline, service description, and call-to-action button
* Emergency service information and business highlights
* Residential and commercial electrical services section
* Full range of electrical services
* Company statistics and performance highlights
* Project portfolio showcasing completed electrical work
* Business certifications and service quality section
* Customer testimonials
* Recent articles and electrical tips
* Service scheduling contact form
* Final call-to-action section
* Footer with navigation, contact information, and company details

## Design Style

The website follows a clean, modern, and industrial design style. The dark background creates a professional appearance, while the red accent color is used for buttons, headings, highlights, and important interactive elements.
