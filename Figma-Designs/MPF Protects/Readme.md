## MPF Protects – Financial Education & Protection Website (Figma UI/UX)

**MPF Protects** is a modern financial services website concept designed in **Figma** to make financial education and protection feel simple, transparent, and accessible. The design focuses on helping individuals and families better understand their financial options while building confidence around protecting their financial future.

The website is crafted to highlight financial education, protection solutions, resources, guidance, and consultation opportunities through a clear and approachable user experience. Clean layouts, trustworthy visual elements, confident typography, and intuitive navigation create a professional digital presence that prioritizes clarity and accessibility.
