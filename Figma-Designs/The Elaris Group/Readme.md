## The Elaris Group – Therapy, Coaching & Brand Consulting Website (Figma UI/UX)

**The Elaris Group** is a modern personal growth and wellness website concept designed in **Figma** to create a compassionate, empowering, and deeply human digital experience for individuals seeking greater clarity, healing, and alignment. The design brings together therapy, coaching, and intuitive brand consulting to support people navigating trauma, anxiety, identity, personal growth, and professional success.

The website is crafted to highlight therapy services, coaching programs, brand consulting, personal transformation resources, practitioner expertise, and client experiences while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Calm visuals, thoughtful layouts, expressive typography, and intuitive navigation create a reassuring journey that encourages visitors to reconnect with themselves and move forward with greater confidence and clarity.
