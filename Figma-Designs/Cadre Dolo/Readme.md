# Cadre Dolo

A modern dark-themed artist website designed for Cadre Dolo, featuring a bold visual style with black, white, and gold accents.

## Overview

The website presents Cadre Dolo's music, story, releases, merchandise, and creative work through a premium editorial-style layout.

## Features

* Responsive artist portfolio website
* Dark and gold visual theme
* Hero section with artist introduction and primary CTA
* Music releases and featured tracks
* Artist story and biography section
* Music and instrumental showcase
* Merchandise section
* Featured lifestyle and promotional content
* Social media and movement section
* Contact and newsletter area
* Structured footer navigation

## Sections

* Home
* Music
* About
* Story
* Releases
* Merchandise
* Gallery
* Contact

## Design

The design uses a dark cinematic aesthetic with bold typography, high-contrast imagery, gold highlights, and spacious layouts to create a premium music-industry feel.
