# In & Out GSE Mechanical Solutions

A professional aviation ground support equipment website designed for In & Out GSE Mechanical Solutions. The website focuses on aircraft maintenance, mechanical solutions, repair services, and ground support equipment.

## Overview

The website presents the company as an experienced aviation maintenance and GSE service provider. The design uses a clean, industrial, and professional visual style with dark tones, aircraft imagery, and structured content sections.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong aviation background image, headline, supporting text, and request quote CTA
* Company introduction and mechanical expertise section
* Aircraft maintenance and repair service information
* GSE repair maintenance services grid
* Services including tugs, baggage carts, belt loaders, fuel trucks, hydraulic systems, power units, troubleshooting, and electrical systems
* Modern aviation industry introduction section
* Project showcase highlighting completed aviation maintenance work
* Project categories such as engine overhaul, system upgrade, and component replacement
* Results and performance section featuring project statistics
* Customer testimonial section
* Final CTA encouraging users to request a quote
* Contact information and newsletter section in the footer

## Design Style

* Professional aviation and industrial aesthetic
* Dark charcoal and white color palette
* Large aircraft and maintenance photography
* Clean typography and structured layouts
* Card-based service presentation
* Strong contrast between content and imagery
* Minimal and corporate visual approach
* Responsive layout designed for desktop and mobile users
