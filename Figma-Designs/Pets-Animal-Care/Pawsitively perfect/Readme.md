# Pawsitively Perfect

Pawsitively Perfect is a modern and friendly pet care website designed to showcase professional pet care services and make it easy for customers to get in touch.

## Features

* Responsive pet care website layout
* Hero section with clear call-to-action
* Pet care services showcase
* Service cards with images and descriptions
* About and pet care information sections
* Pet care team and support highlights
* Customer contact form
* Social media section
* Appointment call-to-action sections
* Clean and playful visual design
* Fully responsive layout for different screen sizes

## Sections

* Hero
* Services
* About Pet Care
* Our Portrait
* Why Choose Us
* Contact
* Social Media
* Appointment CTA
* Footer

## Design

The website uses a soft lavender background, white content sections, orange accent buttons, and purple decorative elements. Rounded cards, pet imagery, and friendly typography create a warm and approachable experience for pet owners.



## Purpose

The website is designed to build trust with pet owners, present available services clearly, and encourage visitors to schedule an appointment or contact the pet care team.
