# Zona’s Holistic Pet Nutrition

A clean and welcoming pet nutrition website designed to promote holistic wellness, personalized nutrition plans, and professional pet care services. The website uses a fresh green and white visual style with friendly pet imagery to create a natural, trustworthy, and approachable experience for pet owners.

## Overview

The website presents Zona’s Holistic Pet Nutrition as a professional pet wellness service focused on improving pets’ health through personalized nutrition and holistic care. The layout is structured to clearly introduce the brand, explain its services, establish credibility, showcase upcoming products, and encourage visitors to book a consultation.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with strong wellness-focused messaging and consultation CTA
* Brand introduction and About Us section
* Holistic approach, expert nutrition, and pet care highlights
* Key benefits including 24/7 pet care, expert veterinarians, and premium pet food
* Services grid featuring nutrition plans, consultations, supplements, wellness programs, and health guidance
* Why Choose Us section highlighting experience and natural ingredients
* Future pet products and waitlist promotion
* Appointment booking form
* Footer with navigation, company information, and contact details

## Design Style

The design follows a modern, minimal, and pet-friendly approach. Soft green tones, white backgrounds, rounded cards, subtle decorative elements, and high-quality pet photography create a calm and natural visual identity. Clear typography and generous spacing make the website easy to browse while green CTA buttons keep important actions prominent.

## Purpose

The main purpose of the website is to build trust with pet owners, communicate the benefits of holistic pet nutrition, showcase available services, and convert visitors into consultation bookings. The website also prepares the brand for future expansion into pet nutrition products.
