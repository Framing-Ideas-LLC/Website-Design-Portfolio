## Urban Oasis Pet Grooming

Urban Oasis Pet Grooming is a modern and clean website design created for a mobile pet grooming service. The website focuses on promoting stress-free, one-on-one grooming experiences for pets and providing an easy way for pet owners to book an appointment.

The design uses a soft white background with light blue sections and deep purple accents to create a friendly, professional, and trustworthy look. Real pet photography is used throughout the website to create a warm and engaging experience.

### Key Sections

* Header with navigation and booking call-to-action
* Hero section highlighting stress-free mobile pet grooming
* About section explaining the one-on-one grooming service
* Service and grooming transformation gallery
* Benefits section explaining why pet owners choose the service
* Simple four-step grooming process
* Appointment call-to-action section
* Customer testimonials slider
* Frequently asked questions section
* Contact and information-focused footer


### Design Focus

The website is designed with a clean layout, strong visual hierarchy, clear call-to-action buttons, pet-focused imagery, and a friendly color palette. The overall experience helps users quickly understand the service, explore grooming options, build trust through testimonials, and easily book an appointment.
