## C&D Behavioural Health – Telehealth Mental Health Website (Figma UI/UX)

**C&D Behavioural Health** is a modern telehealth website concept designed in **Figma** to make compassionate mental health care more accessible to people of all ages. The design focuses on creating a welcoming, private, and user-centered digital experience where individuals can explore personalized behavioral health services and connect with professional support from the comfort of home.

The website is crafted to highlight behavioral health services, telehealth care, treatment areas, provider information, and appointment options while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Calm visuals, accessible layouts, clear information hierarchy, and intuitive navigation create a supportive journey for individuals seeking care tailored to their unique needs.
