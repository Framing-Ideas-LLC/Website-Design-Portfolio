## Unity Home Care – In-Home Care Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Unity Home Care, LLC** is a compassionate home care service created around the belief that everyone deserves to feel valued, respected, safe, and cared for. This website concept was designed to communicate that philosophy through a warm, welcoming, and trustworthy digital experience that helps individuals and families understand available care and feel confident reaching out for support.

The design focuses on making care information easy to understand while creating an emotional connection through compassionate messaging, approachable imagery, clear service presentation, and intuitive navigation. The overall visual direction combines warmth, professionalism, accessibility, and human-centered storytelling to reflect the personal nature of in-home care.
