## Ducks in Row Human Services Inc. – Human Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Ducks in Row Human Services Inc.** is a modern human services website concept designed in **Figma** to highlight community leadership, healthcare expertise, advocacy, and social impact. Led by Founder and CEO **Dr. Genene Duran**, the organization brings together extensive experience in human services, healthcare management, community engagement, health equity, and systems change.

The website is crafted to showcase the organization's mission, leadership, community initiatives, professional expertise, resources, and impact while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Clean layouts, purposeful typography, approachable visuals, and strong storytelling create an engaging digital experience that communicates credibility, compassion, and a commitment to creating healthier and more equitable communities.
