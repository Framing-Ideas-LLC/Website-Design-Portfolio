# Home Care Services Website

A modern and responsive home care services website designed to provide a warm, trustworthy, and professional experience for individuals and families looking for quality in-home care.

## Features

* Clean and responsive website layout
* Hero section with appointment and video CTAs
* Home care service introduction
* About Us section
* Dedicated home care services section
* Service cards with supporting imagery
* Independence and lifestyle-focused content
* Customer satisfaction section
* Contact and appointment form
* Contact details and service information
* Newsletter subscription section
* Responsive footer navigation

## Sections

* Home
* Who We Are
* Services
* Free Assessment
* Why Choose Us
* Contact Us
* Newsletter

## Design

The design uses a clean healthcare-focused layout with a light background, blue accent color, large photography, clear typography, and structured content sections to create a professional and welcoming feel.



## Purpose

The website is designed to help home care providers present their services, build trust with visitors, explain their approach to care, and make it easy for potential clients to request an assessment or get in touch.
