## Space City Radiology PLLC – Landing Page

A modern and professional landing page designed for Space City Radiology PLLC, focused on presenting radiology and diagnostic imaging services in a clear and trustworthy way.

The design uses a clean healthcare-focused layout with strong typography, blue accent colors, structured content sections, and medical imagery to create a professional and user-friendly experience.

### Key Sections

* Hero section with primary call-to-action
* About and radiological care introduction
* Radiology services overview
* Service areas and patient information
* Benefits and key advantages
* Patient testimonial
* Statistics and performance highlights
* Case studies
* Appointment booking form
* Contact information and footer

### Design Approach

The landing page focuses on simplicity, readability, and easy navigation. Content is organized into clear sections with consistent spacing and visual hierarchy, helping patients quickly understand the available services and book an appointment.
