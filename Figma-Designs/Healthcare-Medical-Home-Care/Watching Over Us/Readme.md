## Watching Over Us – In-Home Care Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Watching Over Us** is a modern in-home care website concept designed in **Figma** to provide a compassionate, trustworthy, and family-focused digital experience for individuals seeking dependable non-medical care at home. The design focuses on helping seniors, disabled adults, and individuals recovering from surgery, illness, or injury maintain comfort, dignity, and independence in their familiar surroundings.

The website is crafted to highlight personal care, homemaking services, daily living assistance, flexible care schedules, caregiver support, and family resources while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm visuals, calming layouts, accessible typography, and intuitive navigation create a reassuring journey for individuals and families looking for reliable in-home support.
