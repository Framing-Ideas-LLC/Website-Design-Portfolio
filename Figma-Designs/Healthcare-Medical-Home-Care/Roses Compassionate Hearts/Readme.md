## Roses Compassionate Hearts Home Care – Personalized Home Care Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Roses Compassionate Hearts Home Care** is a warm and compassionate home care website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase personalized, non-medical care services that help individuals remain comfortable, supported, and independent in the place they call home.

The website is crafted to communicate trust, compassion, dignity, and dependable support while clearly presenting available care services, caregiver expertise, client resources, and family support options. Warm photography, calming layouts, accessible typography, and reassuring calls to action create a welcoming digital experience designed to give families confidence when choosing care for their loved ones.
