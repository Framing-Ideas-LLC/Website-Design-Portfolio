## The Potters Home Health Care Agency

A modern and professional healthcare agency website designed to present compassionate in-home care services in a clear, trustworthy, and accessible way. The website focuses on helping families quickly understand available care options, learn about the agency, and request a consultation.

### Key Features

* Responsive and user-friendly website layout
* Professional home healthcare focused design
* Hero section with clear call-to-action buttons
* Home care services showcase
* Rehabilitation, disability, and state-of-the-art care services
* Dedicated staff and care quality section
* Multiple care options for elderly and adult patients
* Meal preparation and travel companion services
* Laundry, linen, bathing, dressing, and grooming assistance
* Service categories with supporting imagery
* Free consultation call-to-action section
* About Us section with agency information
* Statistics and trust indicators
* Home care employment application form
* Mission and goals presentation
* Contact information and footer navigation
* Consistent red, teal, white, and light gray visual theme

### Purpose

The website is built to establish trust and make it easier for individuals and families to find reliable home healthcare services. Its structured content, healthcare imagery, service information, and prominent calls to action guide visitors toward requesting care or contacting the agency.

### Design Style

Clean, professional, and healthcare-focused with a combination of teal and red accents, spacious layouts, rounded content cards, large imagery, and clear typography. The overall design communicates care, reliability, comfort, and professionalism.
