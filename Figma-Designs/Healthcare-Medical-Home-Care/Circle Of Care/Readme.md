# Circle Of Care

A warm, compassionate website design for a non-medical companion care service based in Frederick, Maryland.

## Overview

Circle Of Care provides personalized companion care and daily living support for seniors and families. The website focuses on trust, connection, reliability, and compassionate care.

## Key Features

* Hero section with clear messaging and call-to-action
* About the company and caregiving approach
* Companion care services overview
* Trust and support section with key benefits
* Family testimonials
* Contact and consultation section
* Service area and contact information
* Responsive and user-friendly layout

## Services Highlighted

* Companionship And Conversation
* Errands And Shopping
* Appointment Assistance
* Staying With Your Loved One
* Light Daily Assistance

## Design Style

* Warm and welcoming visual direction
* Soft cream and white backgrounds
* Olive green as the primary brand color
* Muted rose accents for highlights and CTAs
* Elegant serif typography combined with clean supporting text
* Rounded cards and soft imagery
* Senior-care focused photography
* Clean, spacious, and trustworthy layout
