## Committed Life – Church & Community Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Committed Life** is more than a place of worship. It is a faith-centered community built around connection, spiritual growth, purpose, and serving others. Under the leadership of **Bishop N.D. & Lady Marietha Timmerman**, the website concept was designed to create a welcoming digital space where visitors can learn about the church, explore its mission, connect with the community, and discover opportunities for spiritual growth.

The design combines a warm, modern, and inspirational visual direction with clear content organization and intuitive navigation. Strong imagery, meaningful messaging, engaging typography, community-focused sections, and prominent calls to action create an inviting experience for both existing members and people discovering Committed Life for the first time.
