# MJPF – Moving People Forward

A modern financial services website designed for MJPF (Moving People Forward), focused on helping families and businesses build long-term financial security.

## Overview

The website uses a premium dark and gold visual style to create a professional, trustworthy, and established brand presence. The layout combines strong typography, clear messaging, service sections, testimonials, and multiple calls to action.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong brand message and primary CTA
* Introduction section highlighting financial planning
* Services section covering life insurance, income protection, wealth building, financial education, business protection, and legacy planning
* Brand philosophy and financial strategy statement
* Differentiation section explaining the company's approach
* Step-by-step process section
* Client testimonials
* Final CTA section focused on protecting the family
* Contact section with inquiry form and business details
* Minimal footer with navigation

## Design Features

* Dark black background with gold accents
* Elegant serif typography combined with clean supporting text
* Large editorial-style headings
* High-contrast content sections
* Structured card-based service layout
* Professional lifestyle and business imagery
* Clear CTA placement throughout the page
* Responsive and conversion-focused layout



## Purpose

The website is designed to communicate trust, financial expertise, and long-term value while guiding visitors toward exploring services, learning about the company, and getting in touch.
