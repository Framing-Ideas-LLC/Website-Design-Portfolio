# R.E.A.C.H Behavioral Health Center

A modern and professional healthcare website designed for R.E.A.C.H Behavioral Health Center, focused on youth and adult behavioral health services.

## Overview

The website provides clear information about behavioral healthcare services, residential programs, group counseling, family therapy, respite care, and psychiatric support.

The design uses a clean and trustworthy layout with a combination of dark navy, white, and teal accents to create a professional healthcare-focused visual identity.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a consultation request form
* Behavioral health service information
* About R.E.A.C.H section
* Service categories and healthcare programs
* Reach Youth Housing call-to-action
* Downloadable consent and client documents
* Contact information and message form
* Accreditation and provider details
