### Nonprofit - Community - Public Sector

A collection of impactful UI/UX designs created for nonprofit organizations, charities, foundations, community initiatives, and public sector institutions. Each project is designed to communicate purpose, build trust, and encourage engagement through clean layouts, intuitive navigation, and accessible user experiences. From promoting community programs and public services to supporting donations, volunteer participation, and outreach initiatives, these designs help organizations expand their impact and connect with the communities they serve.
