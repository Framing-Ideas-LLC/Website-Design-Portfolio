# ALCD Website

A modern nonprofit website designed for the Academy for Learning and Character Development (ALCD), focused on supporting children, families, and the local community.

## Overview

The website presents ALCD's mission, childcare programs, community initiatives, donation opportunities, and partnerships in a warm and family-friendly layout. The design uses soft neutral backgrounds, green accents, rounded sections, and authentic photography to create a welcoming and trustworthy experience.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with ALCD's mission statement and primary call-to-action
* Mission statement highlighting safe, affordable, and high-quality childcare
* Family support section explaining available opportunities and services
* Donation section with multiple giving options
* Parent testimonial section with supporting video content
* Community impact section showcasing children and family activities
* Future Leaders donation section
* Community Champion section highlighting ways to support ALCD
* Full-width donation call-to-action
* Community gallery featuring real events and activities
* Community partners section displaying supporting organizations
* Footer with navigation and organization information

## Design Features

* Clean and responsive nonprofit-focused design
* Soft cream and light green color palette
* Green used for buttons, highlights, and calls-to-action
* Rounded cards and image containers
* Organic curved section transitions
* Large lifestyle and community photography
* Clear typography hierarchy
* Donation-focused conversion sections
* Mobile-friendly single-column layout
