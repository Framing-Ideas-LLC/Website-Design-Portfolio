## Dear Encourager Community Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Dear Encourager** is a modern community-focused website concept designed in **Figma** to create a safe, supportive, and welcoming digital space for individuals navigating chronic illness experiences. The design focuses on connection, storytelling, and emotional support by encouraging people to share their journeys, find understanding, and feel less alone.

The website is crafted to support personal stories, community discussions, awareness resources, and encouragement-focused content while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm visuals, thoughtful layouts, intuitive navigation, and a compassionate design approach create an engaging experience for individuals seeking connection, empathy, and shared experiences.
