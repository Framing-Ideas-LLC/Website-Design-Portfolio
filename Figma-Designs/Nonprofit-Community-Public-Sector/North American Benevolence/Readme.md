## North American Benevolence Society (NABS) – Family & Community Nonprofit Website (Figma UI/UX)

**North American Benevolence Society (NABS)** is a purpose-driven nonprofit website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase an organization committed to strengthening communities by investing in families, supporting parents, and empowering youth. The design reflects NABS's holistic approach to family development through faith-based coaching, personalized guidance, community outreach, and family-centered programs.

The website is crafted to clearly communicate NABS's mission, coaching services, community initiatives, youth programs, events, and opportunities for involvement. Warm photography, welcoming typography, thoughtful layouts, and community-focused storytelling create an approachable digital experience built around connection, family, service, and positive growth.
