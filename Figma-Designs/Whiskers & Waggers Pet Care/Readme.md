## Whiskers & Waggers – Pet Care Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Whiskers & Waggers** is a modern pet care website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase reliable, compassionate, and professional care for pets across Fairfield and Suisun. As a licensed and insured pet care business, the design focuses on building trust with pet owners while creating a warm and welcoming digital experience for families and their beloved companions.

The website is crafted to highlight pet care services, sitter information, service areas, booking options, and client experiences while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Playful visuals, friendly layouts, intuitive navigation, and pet-focused storytelling create an engaging journey for pet owners looking for dependable care for their furry family members.
