## Clean Sweep HTX – Cleaning Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Clean Sweep HTX** is a modern cleaning services website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase professional cleaning solutions through a trustworthy, welcoming, and service-focused digital experience. As a woman-owned, veteran-led company, the brand emphasizes quality, integrity, diversity, community, and exceptional attention to detail.

The website is crafted to highlight residential and commercial cleaning services, service packages, company values, customer experiences, and booking options while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Clean layouts, fresh visuals, intuitive navigation, and conversion-focused user flows create an engaging journey that helps customers quickly discover services and request professional cleaning support.
