## Precious Jewel – In-Home Care Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Precious Jewel** is a modern in-home care website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase compassionate home care, daily living assistance, and personalized support for individuals and their loved ones. The design focuses on creating a warm, trustworthy, and accessible digital experience that helps families find dependable care while supporting independence and dignity at home.

The website is crafted to highlight in-home care services, personal assistance, caregiver support, care options, and family resources while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm visuals, calming layouts, intuitive navigation, and human-centered storytelling create a welcoming journey for individuals and families looking for reliable support and compassionate care.
