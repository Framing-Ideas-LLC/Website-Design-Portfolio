# Cleaning Products Website

A modern and responsive website designed for a cleaning products and detergent brand. The website focuses on showcasing products, building customer trust, and providing a simple shopping experience.

## Features

* Hero section with promotional messaging and product imagery
* Product showcase with different bead and detergent products
* Product categories and featured products
* Customer testimonial section
* Contact information and social media links
* Newsletter subscription section
* Responsive layout for different screen sizes
* Clean and soft visual design with a light blue color palette
* Simple navigation with clear calls to action
* Footer with quick links, contact details, and policies

## Sections

* Header and Navigation
* Hero Banner
* Product Collection
* Services
* Customer Testimonials
* Contact Section
* Footer

## Design

The website uses a soft blue background combined with cream-colored content cards and rounded elements. Product photography is used throughout the layout to create a product-focused and approachable experience.
