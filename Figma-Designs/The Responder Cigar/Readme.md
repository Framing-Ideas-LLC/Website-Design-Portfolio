# The Responder Cigars Website

## Overview

A premium cigar and lifestyle website designed for Responder Cigars, with a strong focus on first responders, veterans, craftsmanship, and community.

## Key Features

* Bold hero section with a strong brand message and primary call-to-action
* Dedicated section introducing the founder and brand story
* Responder apparel and cigar product categories
* Premium cigar craftsmanship and quality-focused content
* Brand statistics and trust-building information
* Community-focused messaging for first responders and veterans
* Customer testimonials section
* Newsletter signup and community membership section
* Structured footer with navigation, customer help, and company links

## Design

* Dark, premium visual style
* Black and charcoal backgrounds with red accent colors
* Large bold typography for headings
* High-quality cigar, lifestyle, and first responder imagery
* Strong contrast between text and background
* Editorial-style layouts with overlapping images
* Product-focused sections designed to create a luxury brand feel
