# Transcendent Supply LLC

A modern wholesale e-commerce website designed for Transcendent Supply LLC, a supplier and distributor offering a wide range of products for retailers and businesses.

## Overview

The website provides a clean and professional shopping experience with a strong focus on wholesale purchasing, product discovery, and business inquiries. The design combines a dark navy, orange, and white color palette with large product imagery and clear content sections.

## Key Features

* Responsive homepage layout
* Promotional announcement bar
* Hero section with wholesale-focused messaging
* Shop and wholesale inquiry call-to-action buttons
* Business statistics and company highlights
* Product categories section
* Retailer and distributor information section
* Weekly bestseller product showcase
* Product cards with pricing and sale information
* Company benefits and service highlights
* Bulk order and business inquiry section
* Customer testimonials
* Newsletter subscription section
* Simple navigation with Shop, Categories, About, and Contact pages
* Login and account access
* Detailed footer with company, support, and shopping links

## Design

The website uses a clean e-commerce layout with:

* Dark navy backgrounds for important sections
* Orange accents for buttons and highlights
* White backgrounds for product and content areas
* Large rounded product and lifestyle images
* Serif headings combined with clean supporting typography
* Spacious layouts with clear visual hierarchy
* Card-based product and service sections

## Main Sections

1. Hero Banner
2. Company Statistics
3. Product Categories
4. Retailer and Distributor Benefits
5. Weekly Bestsellers
6. Company Benefits
7. Business Bulk Order CTA
8. Customer Testimonials
9. Newsletter Signup
10. Footer Navigation

## Purpose

The website is designed to help retailers and businesses easily discover products, understand wholesale benefits, explore bestsellers, and submit inquiries for bulk purchasing.
