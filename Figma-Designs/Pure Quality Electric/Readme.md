## Pure Quality Electric – Residential & Commercial Electrical Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Pure Quality Electric** is a modern electrical services website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase trusted and professional electrical solutions for residential and commercial customers. The design focuses on communicating reliability, safety, quality workmanship, and dependable service while making it easy for customers to find the right electrical support in their area.

The website is crafted to highlight electrical services, residential solutions, commercial electrical work, repairs, installations, maintenance, emergency support, and service inquiries while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Clean layouts, professional visuals, strong content hierarchy, and intuitive navigation create a trustworthy digital experience for homeowners and businesses.
