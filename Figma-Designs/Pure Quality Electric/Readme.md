# Pure Quality Electric Website

A clean and professional website designed for an electrical services company. The layout focuses on presenting services, building customer trust, showcasing completed projects, and encouraging visitors to book electrical services.

## Project Overview

The website uses a modern and structured layout with a strong red, black, and white visual style. Clear typography, service imagery, statistics, testimonials, and call-to-action sections make the website easy to navigate and conversion-focused.

## Key Sections

* Header with logo, navigation menu, and booking button
* Hero section with strong headline and primary call-to-action
* Electrical services showcase
* Company introduction and experience section
* Professional estimates and satisfaction highlights
* Business statistics and achievements
* Detailed electrical services section
* Recent electrical projects gallery
* Customer reviews and testimonials
* Contact call-to-action section
* Multi-column footer with navigation and contact information

## Services Highlighted

The website presents key electrical services including:

* New Construction
* Extensions and Build Outs
* Remodeling
* Panel Upgrades
* Rewiring
* New Sockets

## Design Style

The design follows a professional electrical and construction industry aesthetic. Red is used as the main accent color for buttons, highlights, and visual elements, while black and white create strong contrast throughout the page.

Large project photography and electrician-related imagery help communicate the company's services visually without making the layout feel overly complicated.

## User Experience

The page is structured to guide visitors from discovering the company and its services to reviewing experience, completed projects, and customer feedback before reaching booking and contact options.

Clear calls-to-action such as "See Our Work", "View More Services", "View More Reviews", and "Get Started Now" help encourage user interaction.
