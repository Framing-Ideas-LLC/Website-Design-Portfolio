## Powder Kote – Custom Architectural Fabrication Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Powder Kote** is a premium architectural fabrication website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase bespoke metalwork created for coastal homes and commercial developments. As a family-owned fabrication studio, the brand combines precision engineering, skilled craftsmanship, and refined finishing to create hardware that performs as beautifully as it looks.

The website is crafted to highlight custom handrails, balustrades, gates, architectural metalwork, fabrication capabilities, and completed projects while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Strong architectural photography, refined typography, industrial-inspired details, and sophisticated layouts create a premium digital experience that communicates craftsmanship, durability, and attention to detail.
