# GodlyHer

GodlyHer is a modern fashion and lifestyle eCommerce website designed for women. The website features a clean editorial layout with a sophisticated visual style, combining fashion, accessories, jewelry, inspirational content, and community engagement.

## Overview

The website provides a complete online shopping experience with curated product categories, featured collections, inspirational content, and newsletter signup functionality.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong brand message and featured visual
* Curated product categories for Gifts, Jewelry, Books & Devotionals, Apparel, and Accessories
* Featured collection section with promotional content
* Featured products displayed in a clean product grid
* Inspirational business and lifestyle banner
* Blog section featuring fashion, beauty, and lifestyle articles
* Social media gallery section
* Community and newsletter subscription section
* Multi-column footer with navigation and company information

## Design Style

The design uses a soft neutral color palette combined with deep burgundy tones to create a premium, elegant, and feminine appearance. Large editorial typography, serif fonts, spacious layouts, and high-quality imagery help create a polished shopping experience.

## Purpose

The project focuses on creating a visually engaging eCommerce platform where users can explore curated fashion and lifestyle products while connecting with inspirational content and the GodlyHer community.
