## Milk & Honey Ice Cream – Artisan Ice Cream E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Milk & Honey Ice Cream** is a modern ice cream website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase handcrafted frozen treats made with natural ingredients, care, and a commitment to exceptional flavor. The brand combines small-batch craftsmanship with a heartfelt belief that great ice cream brings people together and creates moments worth remembering.

The website is crafted to highlight signature ice cream flavors, seasonal offerings, product collections, the brand story, founder's message, and ordering options while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm visuals, appetizing product photography, inviting layouts, and intuitive navigation create an engaging journey that captures the wholesome spirit and handcrafted quality behind every scoop.
