## Luxury Safari Uganda – Safari & Travel Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Luxury Safari Uganda** is a premium travel website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase unforgettable wildlife experiences and luxury adventures across Uganda. The design focuses on inspiring travelers to explore Uganda's breathtaking national parks while creating a sophisticated digital experience that reflects the exclusivity, excitement, and beauty of an African safari.

The website is crafted to highlight game drives, national parks, wildlife encounters, safari packages, guided experiences, accommodations, and travel information while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Immersive photography, elegant layouts, intuitive navigation, and destination-focused storytelling create an engaging journey for travelers planning their next African adventure.

* Premium Safari & Travel UI/UX Design

This concept demonstrates how thoughtful design, immersive storytelling, and powerful visual experiences can help a luxury safari company inspire travelers, showcase Uganda's incredible wildlife destinations, and transform trip discovery into an exciting journey from the very first interaction.

**Tools:** Figma • UI/UX Design • Auto Layout • Design System • Interactive Prototype • Responsive Web Design
