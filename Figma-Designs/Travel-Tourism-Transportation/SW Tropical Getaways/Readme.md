## STG Travel – Tropical Getaways Website (Figma UI/UX)

**SwTropicalGetaways (STG Travel)** is a modern travel website concept designed in **Figma** to inspire unforgettable adventures and simplify the travel planning experience for modern explorers. Founded in 2021 by Shanice Walters, STG Travel was created with a vision to help millennials discover the world through personalized, seamless, and memorable travel experiences.

The website is crafted to showcase tropical destinations, curated travel experiences, vacation packages, trip planning services, and destination inspiration while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Vibrant visuals, immersive layouts, intuitive navigation, and destination-focused storytelling create an engaging journey that inspires visitors to explore, plan, and book their next getaway.


### Project Highlights

* Modern Travel & Tourism UI/UX Design
* Vibrant & Adventure-Focused Brand Experience
* Fully Responsive Layout
* Tropical Destinations & Getaways Showcase
* Curated Vacation & Travel Packages
* Destination Guides & Travel Inspiration
* Personalized Trip Planning Services
* Travel Experience & Activity Sections
* Customer Testimonials & Travel Stories
* Trip Inquiry & Booking Features
* High-Fidelity Figma Prototype
* SEO & Performance-Oriented Structure
* Development-Ready Design System

This concept demonstrates how thoughtful design, immersive visuals, and intuitive navigation can help a travel brand inspire modern explorers, simplify trip planning, and transform destination discovery into an exciting and memorable digital experience.

**Tools:** Figma • UI/UX Design • Auto Layout • Design System • Interactive Prototype • Responsive Web Design
