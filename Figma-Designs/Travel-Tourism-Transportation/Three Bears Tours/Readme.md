## Three Bears Tours – Personalized Hiking & Adventure Tours Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Three Bears Tours** is a welcoming adventure travel website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase personalized hiking and outdoor experiences tailored to each traveler's preferred pace, distance, and level of adventure. The brand brings a playful yet professional approach to guided tours, helping travelers find an experience that feels just right for them.

The website is crafted to highlight Papa Bear treks, Mama Bear wanders, and Baby Bear rambles while showcasing local guides, scenic destinations, tour experiences, difficulty levels, and booking options. Immersive outdoor photography, earthy visual elements, friendly typography, and adventure-focused layouts create an engaging digital experience that captures the spirit of exploration.
