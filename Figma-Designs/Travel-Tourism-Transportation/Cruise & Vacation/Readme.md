## Cruise & Vacation Website

A modern and premium cruise travel website designed to help users discover, compare, and book memorable cruise experiences. The design combines a luxury nautical aesthetic with clear navigation, strong imagery, and conversion-focused sections.

### Key Features

* Full-width hero section with cruise imagery and primary call-to-action
* Cruise search and booking form with destination, date, and traveler filters
* Featured cruise destinations and travel experiences
* About section highlighting company experience and expertise
* Statistics section showcasing bookings, repeat customers, recommendations, and satisfaction
* "Why Choose Us" section highlighting cruise experts, trusted service, and expert guidance
* Large promotional banner for cruise vacation packages
* Latest cruise news and travel updates
* Customer testimonial section
* Structured footer with quick links, company information, and contact details
* Responsive layout suitable for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

### Design Style

The website uses a clean luxury travel aesthetic with deep navy blue, ocean blue, white, and light blue tones. Large cruise photography creates a strong visual focus, while serif-style headings give the website an elegant and premium feel. Light backgrounds and generous spacing keep the content easy to browse.

### Purpose

The main purpose of the website is to provide travelers with an easy way to explore cruise destinations, learn about available experiences, and start planning or booking their next cruise vacation.
