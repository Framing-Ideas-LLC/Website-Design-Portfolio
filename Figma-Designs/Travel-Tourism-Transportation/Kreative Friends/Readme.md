## Kreative Friends Transportation Services – Party Bus & Event Transportation Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Kreative Friends Transportation Services** is a modern transportation website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase premium party bus and event transportation experiences for groups, celebrations, and special occasions. Established in 2024, the brand carries a meaningful connection to founder Adrienne's late son, Kevin Lee Pope, with the "K" in Kreative honoring his memory and the long-standing friendship behind the business.

The website is crafted to highlight party bus services, special events, group transportation, premium amenities, and customized experiences while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Bold visuals, celebratory layouts, intuitive navigation, and experience-focused storytelling create an engaging journey for customers planning memorable events with friends, family, or colleagues.
