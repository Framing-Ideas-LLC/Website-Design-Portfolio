## Bayou Massage Therapy – Massage & Wellness Studio Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Bayou Massage Therapy** is a modern wellness website concept designed in **Figma** to create a calming, restorative, and deeply personal digital experience inspired by the culture, beauty, and peaceful spirit of Louisiana. Rooted in values of family, love, tenderness, and care, the brand aims to bring the same sense of tranquility and connection into every massage experience.

The website is crafted to highlight massage therapy services, wellness treatments, the brand story, practitioner expertise, booking options, and client experiences while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm imagery, soothing layouts, natural visual elements, and thoughtful typography create an immersive journey that reflects the peaceful rhythm and cultural richness of the Louisiana bayou.
