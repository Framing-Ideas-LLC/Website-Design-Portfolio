# Cayla Sweet Treats

A modern and playful bakery website designed to showcase handmade desserts, best-selling treats, and the brand's sweet personality. The landing page uses soft pastel colors, product-focused imagery, and clean typography to create a warm and inviting shopping experience.

## Main Sections

* Hero section with a promotional headline, supporting text, CTA, and featured dessert imagery
* Handmade treats section showcasing different product categories
* Sweet Deals section highlighting featured products and promotional content
* Customer Favorites section with best-selling products and category filters
* Bite-sized treats section presenting product highlights with supporting information
* Product feature sections focused on chocolate and signature desserts
* Newsletter signup section for promotions and updates
* FAQ section answering common customer questions
* Contact section with a customer inquiry form
* Instagram gallery displaying recent product imagery
* Footer with brand information, navigation, and social links

## Design

* Soft pastel pink, peach, white, and purple color palette
* Large product photography throughout the page
* Rounded image treatments and organic decorative shapes
* Clean, playful typography
* Spacious layouts with strong visual hierarchy
* Product-focused sections designed to encourage exploration and purchases
