## The Bloom House – Supportive Living Community Website (Figma UI/UX)

**The Bloom House** is a modern supportive living community website concept designed in **Figma** to create a safe, welcoming, and empowering digital experience for individuals seeking a supportive environment where they can live independently and thrive. The design focuses on communicating compassion, community, independence, and access to personalized support.

The website is crafted to highlight supportive living services, personalized resources, community programs, resident experiences, amenities, and support options while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Warm visuals, inviting layouts, accessible typography, and thoughtful navigation create a reassuring journey for residents, families, caregivers, and community partners.
