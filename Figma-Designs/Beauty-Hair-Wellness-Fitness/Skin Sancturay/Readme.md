## Skin Sanctuary – Natural Skincare E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Skin Sanctuary** is a modern natural skincare e-commerce website concept designed in **Figma** to create a thoughtful, wellness-focused shopping experience centered around intentional skincare and natural ingredients. The design reflects the brand's belief that skincare should be more than a product on a shelf. It should become a meaningful part of a customer's self-care journey.

The website is crafted to highlight handcrafted skincare products, natural ingredients, product benefits, skincare routines, and the brand's philosophy while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Soft visuals, clean layouts, natural imagery, elegant typography, and calming interactions create a premium digital experience that communicates care, authenticity, and quality.
