# Sapphire Hair & Beauty

A modern and elegant website design for Sapphire Hair & Beauty, created to showcase the salon, its story, services, and client experience.

The website features a refined editorial layout with soft neutral tones, elegant typography, and carefully placed imagery to create a premium beauty-focused aesthetic.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong introduction and featured salon imagery
* Welcome and personal introduction section
* About the salon and professional background
* Aesthetics and beauty service information
* Training and professional standards section
* Storytelling sections highlighting the salon journey
* Family and team-focused image gallery
* Location and contact information
* Appointment inquiry form
* Client testimonials and feedback
* Newsletter signup section
* Multi-column footer with navigation and recent posts

## Design Approach

The design uses a clean and spacious layout with a combination of serif and script typography to create an elegant and personal feel. Soft beige and cream backgrounds are paired with dark text and subtle gold accents, giving the website a luxurious and welcoming appearance.

Large photography sections and custom image compositions help tell the story of the brand while maintaining a warm and professional user experience.

The main goal of this design is to create a visually engaging salon website that feels personal, premium, and trustworthy while making it easy for visitors to learn about the business, explore its story, and get in touch.
