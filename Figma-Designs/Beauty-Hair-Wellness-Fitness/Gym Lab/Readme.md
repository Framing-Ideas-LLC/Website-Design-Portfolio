# Gym Lab

## Project Description

Gym Lab is a modern e-commerce website designed for a gym wear and fitness clothing brand. The website focuses on bold visuals, strong typography, and a dark athletic aesthetic to reflect strength, confidence, and performance.

The design showcases premium gym apparel through a visually engaging shopping experience, including featured products, lifestyle imagery, Instagram content, customer testimonials, promotional sections, and newsletter signup.

## Key Features

* Modern and responsive e-commerce design
* Bold hero section with strong brand messaging
* Premium gym wear product showcase
* Featured collection and product cards
* Product pricing and size information
* Lifestyle and fitness-focused visual sections
* Instagram gallery integration
* Customer testimonials slider
* Promotional newsletter signup section
* Clear call-to-action buttons
* Responsive layout for different screen sizes
* Dark and minimal footer with essential navigation links

## Design Style

The overall design uses a dark, bold, and energetic visual direction combined with clean white sections and red accent colors. Large typography, fitness imagery, and strong contrast create a premium athletic brand experience focused on performance and everyday gym culture.
