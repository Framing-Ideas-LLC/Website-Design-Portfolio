# SS&Co. Hair, Beauty, Aesthetics & SPMU

A modern and elegant website designed for SS&Co., a professional beauty destination offering hair, beauty, aesthetics, and SPMU services.

## Overview

The website presents the brand through a clean, premium layout with a soft neutral color palette, refined typography, and professional imagery. It focuses on showcasing treatments, training courses, client testimonials, and the overall SS&Co. experience.

## Key Features

* Modern and premium homepage design
* Hair, beauty, aesthetics, and SPMU service sections
* Treatment showcase with image-based cards
* Beauty training and accredited course section
* Client testimonials
* Appointment booking call-to-actions
* Responsive layout for different screen sizes
* Contact and business information
* Clean and consistent visual branding
