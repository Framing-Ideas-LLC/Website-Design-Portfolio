## Cosmetics Website

A modern and elegant cosmetics e-commerce website designed to showcase organic, natural, and skincare products. The layout combines a soft purple color palette with clean typography, product-focused sections, promotional content, and beauty imagery to create a premium shopping experience.

### Key Sections

* Hero section with promotional messaging and primary CTA
* Featured products carousel
* Organic and natural cosmetics introduction
* Promotional beauty banner
* Why Choose Us section
* Product gallery
* New arrivals product cards
* Promotional product feature
* Customer testimonials
* Newsletter subscription
* Footer with navigation and company information

### Design

* Purple and white color scheme
* Clean and minimal layout
* Soft, feminine visual style
* Large beauty and product imagery
* Spacious section layouts
* Product-focused e-commerce presentation
* Responsive-friendly structure

### Features

* Product showcase
* Product sliders and carousels
* Promotional offers
* Product cards with pricing and ratings
* Customer testimonials
* Image gallery
* Newsletter subscription form
* Navigation menu
* Multiple calls to action

### Purpose

The website is designed for a cosmetics and skincare brand selling natural and organic beauty products. It focuses on building brand trust, presenting products clearly, and encouraging visitors to explore and purchase products.
