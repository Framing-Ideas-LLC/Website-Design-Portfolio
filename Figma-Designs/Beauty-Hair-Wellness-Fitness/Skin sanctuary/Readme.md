# Skin Sanctuary

Skin Sanctuary is a modern skincare and beauty e-commerce website designed with a soft, elegant, and premium visual style. The website combines beauty-focused imagery, product presentation, educational content, and community elements to create a complete skincare experience.

## Key Features

* Modern skincare-focused hero section
* Signature products showcase
* Product cards with pricing and product information
* Brand story and wellness-focused content
* Hero ingredients section
* Skincare ingredient education
* Personalized skincare recommendation section
* Customer testimonials
* Skincare stories and tips
* Frequently Asked Questions section
* Community newsletter signup
* Responsive and clean layout
* Soft pink, cream, white, and dark color palette
* Premium typography with editorial-style headings

## Design Style

The design uses a combination of soft pastel tones, dark sections, elegant typography, high-quality skincare imagery, and spacious layouts. The overall visual direction focuses on creating a luxurious, trustworthy, and feminine skincare brand experience.

## Purpose

The website is designed to showcase skincare products while educating visitors about ingredients, building customer trust through reviews, and encouraging visitors to become part of the brand community.
