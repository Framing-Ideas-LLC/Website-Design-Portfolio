# Bayou Massage Therapy

A modern and professional website design for Bayou Massage Therapy, focused on providing personalized massage services in the comfort of the client's home. The website creates a calm and welcoming experience through a clean layout, soft neutral colors, elegant typography, and high-quality imagery.

## Project Overview

The website is designed to showcase personalized massage services and help visitors understand the benefits of each treatment. It combines informative content, service highlights, therapist information, client testimonials, and clear booking options.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong introduction and booking call-to-action
* Personalized massage benefits and treatment highlights
* About section introducing the massage therapist and approach
* Statistics section showing experience and client achievements
* Massage services including relaxation massage, deep tissue therapy, trigger point release, and hot stone massage
* Personalized wellness journey section
* Client testimonials and feedback
* Final call-to-action encouraging users to book a massage session
* Footer with navigation links, legal pages, contact information, and newsletter signup

## Design Approach

The design uses a soft and calming visual style that reflects relaxation, wellness, and professional care. Neutral backgrounds are combined with dark brown accents and elegant serif typography to create a warm and premium appearance.

The layout is structured to guide users naturally from learning about the services to understanding the therapist's experience and finally booking a session. Large lifestyle images help build trust and create a more personal connection with visitors.
