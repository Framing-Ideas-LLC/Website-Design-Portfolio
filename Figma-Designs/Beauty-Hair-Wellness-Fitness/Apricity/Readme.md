# Apricity Wellness and Aesthetics

A modern wellness and aesthetics website designed for Apricity Wellness and Aesthetics PLLC. The website presents aesthetic treatments, wellness services, and personalized care through a clean and elegant visual style.

## Overview

The website focuses on building trust and encouraging visitors to book consultations. It combines warm neutral tones, rounded image layouts, refined typography, and professional healthcare imagery to create a calm and premium experience.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong introduction and consultation CTA
* Wellness and aesthetics introduction
* Business statistics and achievements
* Aesthetic services section
* Nurse practitioner consultation section
* Why choose Apricity section
* Client testimonial
* Consultation/contact form
* Footer with navigation, services, contact details, and social links

## Services

* Regenerative Treatments
* Neurotoxins
* Hormone Therapy
* Weightloss
* Vitamin Injections
* Peptide Therapy

## Design Style

* Clean and elegant healthcare aesthetic
* Warm beige, white, brown, and terracotta color palette
* Large rounded and curved image treatments
* Serif-style display typography combined with clean supporting text
* Spacious layouts with strong visual hierarchy
* Professional photography focused on wellness, skincare, and aesthetics
* Clear consultation-focused calls to action
