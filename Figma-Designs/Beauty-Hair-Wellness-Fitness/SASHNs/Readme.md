## Project Description

Sashn is a modern and professional website designed for a fractional HR business. The website focuses on presenting HR services, business support, company information, and resources in a clean and approachable way.

The design uses a minimal layout with a soft neutral background and dark blue-gray sections to create a professional and trustworthy appearance. The homepage includes a strong hero section, company introduction, founder profile, client logos, service offerings, business insights, blog articles, and a contact form.

## Key Features

* Modern and professional HR-focused design
* Clear hero section with call-to-action buttons
* About and company introduction sections
* Founder and team presentation
* Client and partner logo showcase
* Service offerings with visual cards
* Informational content and business insights
* Statistics and company achievements section
* Recent blogs and resources
* Contact form for inquiries
* Newsletter subscription section
* Fully responsive layout for different screen sizes


## Design Focus

The website is designed to communicate trust, professionalism, and personalized HR support. The clean structure, balanced typography, professional imagery, and organized content sections create an experience that is easy to navigate and suitable for businesses looking for flexible HR solutions.
