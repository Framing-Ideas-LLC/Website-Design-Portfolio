# HC Mobile Notary Services Website

A modern and professional website designed for a Texas-based mobile notary service. The website focuses on building trust, simplifying appointment booking, and clearly presenting the range of notary services available to clients.

## Overview

The website features a clean and elegant layout with a professional visual identity. It combines dark imagery, warm tones, and a blue accent color to create a trustworthy and premium appearance.

The homepage is structured to guide visitors from the main service introduction to service categories, business benefits, frequently asked questions, and appointment booking.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a clear introduction to the mobile notary service and consultation call-to-action.
* About section introducing the business and its commitment to providing reliable notary services.
* Services section showcasing General Notary Work, Loan Signing Services, Real Estate and Legal Support, and Additional Services.
* Why Choose Us section highlighting accuracy, convenience, prompt service, flexible scheduling, confidentiality, and extensive service availability.
* FAQ section answering common questions about mobile notary services and appointment requirements.
* Appointment booking section with a detailed contact form for scheduling notary services.
* Professional footer containing navigation links, service links, contact information, and social media links.

## Design Approach

The design uses a minimal and professional interface with large typography, clean spacing, structured service cards, and high-quality imagery. The visual style is focused on trust, professionalism, convenience, and accessibility.
