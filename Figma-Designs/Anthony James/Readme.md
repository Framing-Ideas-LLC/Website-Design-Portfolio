## Anthony James – Author, Coach & Mentor Website (Figma UI/UX)

**A.J. Leonard** is a modern personal brand website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase the expertise, insights, and professional journey of Anthony James Leonard, widely known as A.J. Leonard. As an author, coach, educator, and mentor, A.J. uses a creative and common-sense approach to help individuals unlock their potential and build more successful and fulfilling personal and professional lives.

The website is crafted to highlight A.J.'s books, coaching services, educational resources, mentorship programs, professional background, insights, and personal philosophy while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Refined visuals, engaging storytelling, clear content hierarchy, and intuitive navigation create an inspiring journey for individuals seeking guidance, personal growth, and professional development.
