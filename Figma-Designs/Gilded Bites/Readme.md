## Gilded Bites by Maria Boura – Artisan Chocolates & Luxury Desserts Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Gilded Bites by Maria Boura** is a luxurious dessert and confectionery website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase handcrafted artisan chocolates, refined desserts, and beautifully curated event presentations. The design positions the brand as an elevated destination for clients seeking sophisticated sweets and memorable culinary experiences for special occasions.

The website is crafted to highlight signature chocolates, dessert collections, custom creations, event presentations, gifting options, and the story behind the brand while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Elegant food photography, luxurious typography, refined layouts, and premium visual details create an indulgent digital experience that reflects the craftsmanship behind every creation.
