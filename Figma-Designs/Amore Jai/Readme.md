# Amore Jai

A premium luxury luggage and travel accessories website designed with an elegant, editorial-style aesthetic. The website focuses on refined visuals, spacious layouts, high-quality travel imagery, and a sophisticated green and cream color palette.

## Features

* Luxury-focused hero section with strong brand messaging
* Product-focused luggage showcase
* Travel lifestyle imagery throughout the page
* Featured product collections
* Product cards with pricing
* Brand value and product quality sections
* Editorial-style promotional sections
* Luxury storytelling content
* Customer reviews section
* Exclusive product drop announcement
* Newsletter signup section
* Responsive footer with navigation and contact information

## Design

* Clean luxury and editorial-inspired layout
* Cream and dark green color palette
* Large serif typography for headings
* Minimal sans-serif typography for supporting content
* Thin borders and spacious content sections
* High-quality travel and lifestyle photography
* Premium product presentation with generous whitespace
* Dark promotional sections for visual contrast
