# Final Touch Barbers & Beauty

A modern barbershop and beauty salon website designed for Final Touch Barbers & Beauty. The website combines a bold black, white, and red visual style with strong typography and high-quality imagery to create a professional and confident brand presence.

## Features

* Modern responsive homepage design
* Hero section with appointment CTA and video play button
* Barbershop and beauty salon service sections
* Service cards with imagery and descriptions
* Hair color and chemical treatment section
* Why Choose Final Touch section
* Appointment booking CTA
* Promotional scrolling service banner
* Gallery and service-focused visual content
* Responsive footer with navigation and business information

## Design

* Bold red, black, and white color palette
* Strong condensed typography for headings
* Clean layouts with large photography
* High-contrast sections for better visual hierarchy
* Professional barbershop and salon aesthetic
* Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices
