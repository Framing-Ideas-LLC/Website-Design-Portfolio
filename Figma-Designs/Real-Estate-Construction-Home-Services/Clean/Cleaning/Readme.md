# Clean Sweep HTX - Cleaning Services Website

## Overview

Clean Sweep HTX is a modern cleaning services website designed for a professional cleaning company serving residential and commercial customers in Houston.

The website focuses on building trust, showcasing cleaning services, highlighting company experience, and encouraging visitors to request a quote or contact the business.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with company introduction and primary call-to-action
* Company guarantee and customer satisfaction section
* Comprehensive cleaning services grid
* Business statistics and achievements
* About Clean Sweep HTX section
* Why Hire Us section with key benefits
* Cleaning project showcase
* Customer testimonial section
* Newsletter signup
* Footer with company information and navigation links

## Design

* Clean and professional visual style
* Light background with blue as the primary accent color
* Large photography focused on cleaning professionals and completed projects
* Rounded cards and image containers
* Clear typography and structured content layout
* Responsive service and project sections
* Strong CTA placement throughout the page

## Services Highlighted

* Residential cleaning
* Commercial cleaning
* Floor cleaning
* House cleaning
* Eco-friendly cleaning
* Customized cleaning plans
* Insured and bonded services
* 24/7 customer service
