## Ray’s Cleaning Services – Residential Cleaning Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Ray’s Cleaning Services** is a modern residential cleaning website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase dependable, flexible, and detail-focused cleaning solutions for homes. The design focuses on making professional cleaning services easy to understand and accessible while communicating cleanliness, reliability, and spotless results.

The website is crafted to highlight recurring cleaning plans, deep cleaning, routine upkeep, service details, pricing information, and booking options while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Fresh visuals, clean layouts, bright imagery, and intuitive navigation create a polished digital experience that reflects the quality and care behind every cleaning service.
