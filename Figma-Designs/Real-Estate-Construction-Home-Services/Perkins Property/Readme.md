## Perkins Property – Property & Lawn Maintenance Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Perkins Property** is a professional property maintenance website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase reliable landscaping, lawn care, handyman, and residential repair services throughout Charlotte, NC. The design positions the company as a dependable local partner for homeowners and property owners who want their spaces maintained, repaired, and looking their best.

The website is crafted to highlight property maintenance services, lawn care, landscaping, handyman solutions, residential repairs, service areas, and customer support while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Clean outdoor imagery, practical layouts, strong typography, and clear calls to action create a trustworthy digital experience built around reliability, professionalism, and local service.
