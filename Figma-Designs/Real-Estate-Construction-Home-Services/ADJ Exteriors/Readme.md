## ADJ Exteriors, Inc. – Roofing & Exterior Services Website (Figma UI/UX)

**ADJ Exteriors, Inc.** is a modern roofing and exterior services website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase dependable craftsmanship, quality materials, and professional home protection solutions. Serving Sugar Grove, Illinois, and surrounding areas, the design focuses on building homeowner confidence through clear communication, reliable service, and attention to detail.

The website is crafted to highlight roofing, siding, exterior improvements, inspections, repairs, project portfolios, and customer support while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Strong property visuals, clean layouts, structured service sections, and intuitive navigation create a professional digital experience for homeowners looking to protect and improve their properties.
