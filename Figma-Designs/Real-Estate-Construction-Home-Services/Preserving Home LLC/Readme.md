# Preserving Home LLC

A modern healthcare training website designed for CNAs and healthcare professionals. The website focuses on CNA training, certification exams, state testing, and professional healthcare education.

## Features

* Clean and professional healthcare-focused design
* Hero section with clear call-to-action
* CNA training and certification services
* About section highlighting experience and expertise
* Service showcase with interactive-style content
* Project and training results section
* Statistics and trust indicators
* Client testimonials section
* Contact and inquiry form
* Responsive layout for different screen sizes
* Structured footer with company information and navigation

## Design

The website uses a clean healthcare aesthetic with a light background, yellow accent color, professional typography, medical imagery, and subtle geometric background elements.
