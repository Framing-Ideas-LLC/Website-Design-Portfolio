## Total Technique Academy – Dance & Performing Arts Website (Figma UI/UX)

**Total Technique Academy** is an inspiring dance and performing arts website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase a training environment where movement becomes a pathway to confidence, creativity, leadership, and self-discovery. The academy goes beyond technical dance training by helping students develop the skills and mindset to confidently take on every stage life presents.

The website is crafted to highlight dance programs, classes, instructors, performance opportunities, student development, and academy values while delivering a seamless experience across all devices. Dynamic photography, expressive typography, energetic layouts, and engaging visual elements create an immersive digital experience that reflects the passion and confidence of the academy.
