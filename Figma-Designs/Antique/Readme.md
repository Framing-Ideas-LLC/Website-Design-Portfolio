# Antique Marketplace

A modern and elegant vintage marketplace website designed to showcase antique products, art pieces, collectibles, and unique vintage items.

## Overview

The website features a clean and sophisticated layout with a neutral color palette that reflects the premium and timeless nature of antique products. The design focuses on product discovery, storytelling, customer engagement, and a smooth browsing experience.

## Key Sections

* Hero section with a strong introduction and call-to-action
* Services section highlighting art curation, art sales, and exhibition spaces
* Featured collection section with vintage product storytelling
* Discover vintage treasures section with category-based cards
* Vintage products showcase with product images and pricing
* Step-by-step section explaining how users can start selling vintage items
* New arrivals section for recently added products
* Authenticity and quality information section
* Customer feedback and testimonials
* Footer with navigation, contact information, and opening hours

## Design Approach

The website uses a minimal and refined visual style with soft neutral backgrounds, elegant typography, warm accent colors, and high-quality product imagery. The layout is designed to create a premium marketplace experience while keeping navigation and product discovery simple.
