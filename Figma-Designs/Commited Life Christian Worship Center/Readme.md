# Church Community Website

A modern and responsive church community website focused on worship, community, leadership, and spiritual growth.

## Overview

The website uses a clean editorial layout with large typography, warm photography, soft cream backgrounds, and gold accents to create a welcoming and premium church experience.

## Main Sections

* Hero section with church message and primary call to action
* Church introduction and community statement
* Worship, Children, Youth, Outreach, and Leadership sections
* Church mission and spiritual growth section
* Worship and weekly gathering information
* Church leadership profiles
* Community testimonials
* Final call-to-action section
* Footer with navigation and social links

## Design

* Minimal and elegant visual style
* Large serif typography for headings
* Gold accent color for highlighted text and buttons
* Warm neutral backgrounds
* Large rounded image containers
* Editorial-style content layout
* Strong use of whitespace
* High-quality church and community photography

## Responsive Design

The layout is designed to adapt across:

* Desktop
* Tablet
* Mobile

Content, images, cards, navigation, and typography should scale and stack appropriately on smaller screens.

## Key Features

* Clear navigation
* Strong visual hierarchy
* Multiple content cards
* Church service information
* Leadership profiles
* Community-focused messaging
* Multiple calls to action
* Responsive image-based sections
* Clean and accessible content structure

The goal of the website is to communicate the church's mission, introduce its community, showcase its ministries and leaders, and encourage visitors to attend, connect, and become part of the church community.
