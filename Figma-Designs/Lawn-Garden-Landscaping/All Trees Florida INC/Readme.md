# All Trees Florida Inc. Website

A modern and professional website designed for a tree care and property maintenance company based in Florida. The website focuses on showcasing the company's services, building trust, and making it easy for customers to request a free estimate.

## Website Overview

The homepage features a strong visual design with nature-inspired imagery, green color tones, and a clean layout. The hero section immediately introduces the company with the headline:

“All Trees Florida Inc. – You Grow It, We Cut It!”

A clear call-to-action encourages users to get a free estimate.

## Sections Included

### Hero Section

A full-width banner with a tree service professional image, company navigation, main heading, short supporting text, and a free estimate button.

### About Section

Introduces the company and its commitment to maintaining and protecting residential and commercial properties throughout Florida. The section uses layered landscaping and tree service images.

### Services Section

Highlights the main services offered by the company:

* Tree Trimming & Removal
* Stump Grinding
* Palm Tree Cleaning
* Sod Installation

Each service is presented in a clean horizontal layout with a short description and navigation option.

### Before and After Section

A visual comparison section showing how professional landscaping and tree services can transform outdoor spaces.

### Why Choose Us Section

Displays the main benefits of working with the company, including:

* Licensed & Insured
* Free Estimates
* Reliable Crew
* Residential & Commercial Services

### Call to Action Section

A visually strong section encouraging visitors to take action before tree problems become more serious, with a direct option to request a free estimate.

### Quote Request Section

A contact form where users can submit their name, phone number, email, property address, preferred date and time, and a message to request a quote.

The section also includes direct contact information and a supporting image of a tree service professional.

### Footer

Includes the company logo, navigation links, email subscription field, copyright information, and website credit.

## Design Style

The website uses a clean, natural, and professional visual style. Dark forest green is combined with white and light neutral backgrounds to create a strong connection with outdoor and landscaping services. Large photography, rounded elements, clean typography, and spacious layouts help create a trustworthy and modern user experience.
