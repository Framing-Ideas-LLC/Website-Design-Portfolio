## TRIO Sports Apparel – E-Commerce Website (Figma UI/UX)

**TRIO Sports Apparel** is a modern e-commerce website concept designed in **Figma** to showcase high-quality sports apparel through a bold, energetic, and conversion-focused online shopping experience. The design focuses on helping athletes and active customers discover performance-driven clothing while creating a strong and memorable digital brand presence.

The website is crafted to highlight sportswear collections, featured products, new arrivals, best sellers, and performance apparel while delivering a seamless shopping experience across all devices. Dynamic visuals, bold typography, intuitive navigation, and streamlined shopping flows create an engaging journey from product discovery to checkout.



### Project Highlights

* Modern Sports Apparel E-Commerce UI/UX Design
* Bold & Performance-Focused Brand Experience
* Fully Responsive Layout
* Sportswear & Apparel Collection Showcase
* New Arrivals & Best Sellers Sections
* Product Categories & Smart Discovery
* Detailed Product Pages & Image Galleries
* Shopping Cart & Streamlined Checkout Flow
* Customer Reviews & Product Ratings
* Promotions & Featured Collection Sections
* High-Fidelity Figma Prototype
* SEO & Performance-Oriented Structure
* Development-Ready Design System

This concept demonstrates how thoughtful e-commerce design, intuitive product discovery, and streamlined user flows can help a sports apparel brand strengthen its digital presence, improve customer engagement, and create a seamless shopping experience from browsing to purchase.

**Tools:** Figma • UI/UX Design • Auto Layout • Design System • Interactive Prototype • Responsive Web Design
