# Haus of London – Luxury Hair Salon Website

A premium and elegant website design for Haus of London, a luxury hair salon specializing in hairstyling, wig installations, and premium hair care.

## Project Overview

The website features a modern luxury aesthetic with a dark theme, elegant typography, warm neutral accents, and high-quality beauty photography.

## Website Sections

- Hero Section
- About Us
- Our Services
- Why Choose Us
- Hair Gallery
- Appointment Booking Form
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Footer

## Design Features

- Premium black and beige color palette
- Elegant serif typography
- Minimal and clean layouts
- Large editorial-style images
- Smooth section transitions
- Responsive website layout
- Clear appointment booking calls to action
- Modern FAQ accordion

## Design Direction

The visual direction focuses on luxury, beauty, sophistication, and simplicity. The website creates a premium salon experience through elegant layouts, refined typography, and professional hair photography.

## Project Goal

To create a professional online presence for Haus of London that showcases its services, highlights its expertise, builds brand trust, and encourages visitors to book appointments.
