# Holistic Health & Wellness – Professional Healthcare Website

Holistic Health & Wellness is a professional healthcare website designed to provide patients with clear, accessible, and compassionate information about integrated wellness services in Baltimore. The website brings together mental health support, primary care, substance-use treatment, and medical cannabis evaluations within a calm and trustworthy digital environment.

The design focuses on simplifying the patient journey through organized service pages, clear navigation, provider information, insurance and payment guidance, frequently asked questions, and strategically placed appointment-booking options. Each section helps visitors understand the available care and quickly find the information needed before scheduling a consultation.

A welcoming visual style, readable content structure, and patient-focused messaging help communicate professionalism, inclusivity, and compassionate care. The website is structured to support both insured and uninsured patients while making essential healthcare information accessible across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
